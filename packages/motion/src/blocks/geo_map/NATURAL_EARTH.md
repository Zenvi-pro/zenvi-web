# Map data

`world.gen.ts` is generated by `packages/motion/scripts/gen-geo.ts` from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:50m Admin 0 Countries and
1:50m Populated Places (GeoJSON from
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector), simplified (Douglas-Peucker,
0.03°) and quantised to 1/100°.

Licence: public domain. "All versions of Natural Earth raster + vector map data
found on this website are in the public domain. You may use the maps in any
manner, including modifying the content and design, electronic dissemination,
and offset printing." (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
Made with Natural Earth.

Borders are Natural Earth's de facto boundaries; disputed areas follow its
defaults, not any one country's view.
