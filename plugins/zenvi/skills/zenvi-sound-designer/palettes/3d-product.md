# 3D product ad (device spins, lands, UI chips)

Design: `auto_sound_design_tool` `{"genre": "product_3d"}` - scene events
(spins, lands, bursts) are on the sheet.
Score / master: `"genre": "3d_product"`.

- Sound design is foregrounded over a minimal, premium bed: weighty air
  whooshes on spins, a soft low impact on the landing one frame late (sub +
  harmonic), servo and mechanical micro details, glints on specular passes,
  a tonal hit on the logo. 0.8-2 cues/s.
- Space: a clean dark hall, 1.2-2 s, 30 ms pre-delay
  (`space_tool` `{"role": "sfx", "space": "hall", "mix": 0.15}`).
- Signature: the landing thump, then near-silence before the logo.
- Avoid: cartoon pops, clutter, a whoosh on a cut with no motion.
