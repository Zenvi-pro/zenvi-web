# Editorial (brutalist grids, dither masks, letter swaps, glitch type)

Design: `auto_sound_design_tool` `{"genre": "editorial"}`.
Score / master: `"genre": "editorial"`.

- Crisp, dry digital ticks; bit-crush or dither bursts on pixel transitions;
  typewriter or relay clicks on letter swaps; one restrained low tone for the
  hero word. 0.8-2 cues/s.
- Minimal, ambient or glitch music, or none; mostly dry, with one long tail
  as a deliberate effect.
- Signature: rhythmic tick patterns that follow the grid builds.
- Avoid: lush reverbs, big subs, stock cinematic whooshes, density past
  2/s (thin it with `intensity: "subtle"` rather than deleting cues by hand).
