# Flash montage (3-6 frame cuts, "evolution of time")

Design: `auto_sound_design_tool` `{"genre": "flash_montage"}`.
Score / master: `"genre": "flash_montage"`.

- Cuts closer than 150 ms are one hit point: the run gets one texture or
  riser and a tick every 2-4 cuts, pitched up across the run; the cuts inside
  stay dry (the sheet leaves them so: do not fill them in).
- 1-3 cues/s overall, music-led; glitch bursts on flash frames; one hero hit
  and a long tail where the run ends and on match beats.
- Signature: an accelerating tick pattern locked to the cut rate, then
  silence, then one big hit.
- Avoid: sounding every cut (machine-gun), the same click repeated, a busy
  bed under the silence before the hit.
