# Match cut (pose, shape and circle chains)

Design: `auto_sound_design_tool` `{"genre": "match_cut"}`.
Score / master: `"genre": "match_cut"`.

- One connecting sound across each match: a whoosh or tone that bridges both
  shots, or a hit whose tail runs into the next shot. Keep one character for
  the whole chain (the same family, pitch-walked), never a different whoosh
  per match.
- 0.8-2 cues/s: the matches, not every cut. Rhythmic music; matches on the
  beat where they fall on it.
- Signature: a sound that belongs to both objects (a clink becoming a hum);
  tonal pings in the music's key for circle chains.
- Avoid: a gap at the match point, a cue between matches, stacking an extra
  add_sfx_tool on every cut (density goes past 2/s).
