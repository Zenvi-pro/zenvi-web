# T001 Aperture fly-through (CGI render)

## What it is

The camera pushes into an opening on the product (a screen, a port, a
grille, a doorway) and comes out the other side in a different scene,
matched in scale and motion. The chapter link of product CGI films (3 of
the 4 reference films use it). One or two per film, between features.

## Build it in Zenvi

Zenvi has no camera path through a model's geometry. The honest version
today goes through a *screen*: `device_mockup` plays the next scene on the
device, the camera pushes in, the clip is scaled into the screen, and a
`zoom_through` hands over to the same footage full frame. Screen content
and the next shot are the same file, and the next shot starts where the
screen's playback ends (`media_start_seconds` + scene length), so the
picture carries through.

```json
add_3d_scene_tool {"scene": "device_mockup", "quality": "draft", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 3, "track": "2", "params": {"device": "laptop", "file_id": "<next scene>", "media_start_seconds": 2, "move": "push", "background": "#07080b", "dof": true, "shadow": true}}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<next scene>", "position_seconds": 3, "start_seconds": 5, "end_seconds": 8.5, "track": "1"}
```

Look at the draft's last frame and read off the screen's centre (here
x 0.48, y 0.40). Pivot on it and accelerate into it over the last second,
then let the incoming shot decelerate from oversize:

```json
set_clip_properties_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<3d clip>"], "properties": {"origin_x": 0.48, "origin_y": 0.40}}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<3d clip>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 2.0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.0, "value": 3.5, "ease": "ease_in_expo"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<3d clip>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 2.0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.0, "value": 3.5, "ease": "ease_in_expo"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<next>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 3.0, "value": 1.25}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.9, "value": 1.0, "ease": "ease_out_cubic"}, {"timeline_seconds": 6.5, "value": 1.05}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<next>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 3.0, "value": 1.25}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.9, "value": 1.0, "ease": "ease_out_cubic"}, {"timeline_seconds": 6.5, "value": 1.05}]}
apply_motion_transition_tool {"preset": "zoom_through", "clip1_id": "<3d clip>", "clip2_id": "<next>", "duration": 0.5, "intensity": 1.5, "focus_x": 0.48, "focus_y": 0.40}
rerender_3d_scene_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<3d clip>", "quality": "final"}
```

```json
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "whoosh_rise", "kit": "synth", "at_seconds": 3.0, "duration": 1.2, "gain_db": -10}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "swish_airy", "at_seconds": 3.0, "gain_db": -14}

```

Fallback without Blender: the same scale-into-a-point move on live
footage of an opening (a tunnel, a doorway, a speaker grille macro):
`set_keyframes` scale with `ease_in_expo` to the opening's centre, then
`zoom_through` with `focus_x`/`focus_y` on it. It reads as a fast push
cut, not a camera passing through geometry.

## Timing & taste rules

- Push 1-1.5 s, accelerating (`ease_in_expo`); the hand-over 0.4-0.6 s
  (`zoom_through` duration 0.5, intensity 1.5); the exit decelerates over
  0.8-1 s from 1.2-1.3x to 1.0.
- The screen must fill the frame *before* the cut: scale the 3D clip until
  the bezel leaves frame (3-3.5x for a laptop at the end of `push`). At
  1.9x the bezel is still in shot and the judge read it as a hard cut.
- Pivot (`origin_x`/`origin_y`) and `focus_x`/`focus_y` on the same point:
  the screen's centre, read off the draft.
- Draft for framing only; always `rerender_3d_scene_tool` `final` before
  judging: the draft is half size and reads as "unfinished".
- Do not end on the exit shot: the next scene must do something (a title,
  a move, the next feature) within 2 s.

## Sound for it

A transition cue, 2-3 layers with the peak on the hand-over frame: a
rising whoosh as long as the push (use `kit: "synth"` so `duration`
holds: the recorded kit whoosh comes back 0.37 s long whatever you ask),
an airy swish on the frame, a soft sub under it. Music keeps running
across the cut.

## Common mistakes

- Cutting while the device is still small in frame: a scale jump, not a
  dive.
- Screen content that is not the next shot (the exit has to be what was on
  the screen).
- Linear scale: the push needs to accelerate in and settle out.
- A thin single whoosh with no low end.

## Verified

2026-10-28. Blender `device_mockup` laptop playing a Pexels night headlight
clip, then the same clip full frame, 6.5 s at 1920x1080; render, contact
sheet and judge in `~/zenvi-motion/fleet/artifacts/sk2-product-brand/techniques/T001.*`.
Pass 1 (draft 3D, cut at small screen size, 0.37 s kit whoosh) 3.4/10:
"pops rather than a fly-through", low-res 3D. Pass 2 (final render, 1.9x
ease-in push into the screen, 1.25x settle on the exit, synth riser, fade)
3.8/10: the dive reads ("the concept is clear") but the judge still wants
the bezel to pass the camera and more motion blur; the recipe now pushes
to 3.5x with `ease_in_expo` (not re-judged). What it cannot do: a real
camera path through a port or grille into a second 3D scene, with true
motion blur; that needs `cgi-aperture-flythrough`.
