# T002 Exploded-view levitation and mockup

## What it is

Parts or clones of the product separate along one axis with staggered
delays (about 4 frames) and an ease-out, hover weightless, then glide back
together and snap flush on a click. Sizes of one accessory, layers of a
device, or widget cards on a page. One per feature, 2-4 s.

## Build it in Zenvi

Zenvi cannot pull a model's sub-meshes apart. What works today is the
*clone* version: render the product once on a transparent background,
stack copies on tracks above each other, and animate them in the editor.
The top track is the centre one (it hides the others when they land).

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<dark backdrop image>", "position_seconds": 0, "end_seconds": 4.0, "track": "1"}
add_3d_scene_tool {"scene": "model_showcase", "quality": "draft", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 4, "track": "2", "params": {"model": "<product .glb, or product: player>", "move": "turntable", "turn_degrees": 40, "stage": "dark", "background": "transparent", "size": 0.2, "shadow": false, "elevation": 12}}
rerender_3d_scene_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<render>", "quality": "final"}
duplicate_clips_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<render>"], "position_seconds": 0, "to_track": "3"}
```

Repeat `duplicate_clips_tool` for tracks 4, 5, 6 (five copies; track 6 is
the centre). Rerender *before* duplicating. Per clone, the same four-beat
curve on `location_x`, `scale_x`, `scale_y` (outer pair shown; inner pair
starts 4 frames earlier, at 0.4 s, to ±0.20 and 0.9):

```json
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<outer left>", "property": "location_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0.533, "value": 0}, {"timeline_seconds": 1.333, "value": -0.40, "ease": "ease_out_expo"}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.4, "value": -0.416, "ease": "ease_in_out_sine"}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.0, "value": 0, "ease": "ease_in_expo"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<outer left>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0.533, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 1.333, "value": 0.8, "ease": "ease_out_expo"}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.4, "value": 0.8}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.0, "value": 1, "ease": "ease_in_expo"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<outer left>", "property": "location_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 1.333, "value": 0}, {"timeline_seconds": 1.87, "value": -0.018, "ease": "ease_in_out_sine"}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.4, "value": 0, "ease": "ease_in_out_sine"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<centre>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 3.0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.06, "value": 1.08, "ease": "ease_out_quad"}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.35, "value": 0.99, "ease": "ease_out_cubic"}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.55, "value": 1.0}]}
```

(`scale_y` takes the same points as `scale_x`.) The snap:

```json
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "swish_airy", "at_seconds": 0.75, "pan": -0.6, "gain_db": -16}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "swish_airy", "at_seconds": 0.9, "pan": 0.6, "gain_db": -16, "seed": 2}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "whoosh_medium", "kit": "synth", "at_seconds": 3.0, "duration": 0.7, "gain_db": -14}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "hit_transient", "at_seconds": 3.0, "gain_db": -6}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "mouse_click", "kit": "synth", "at_seconds": 3.0, "brightness": 0.9, "gain_db": -6}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "impact_thud", "at_seconds": 3.0, "pitch": -3, "gain_db": -12}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

Fallback without Blender: the same clone curves on a product cut-out PNG
(alpha) instead of the render; flatter, but the stagger and snap read the
same. For an abstract burst, `icon_cube` with `explode: true` breaks into
cubelets, but they scatter and never reassemble: it is not this technique.

## Timing & taste rules

- Stagger 4 frames (0.133 s at 30 fps) between pairs, centre out.
- Fan-out 0.8 s `ease_out_expo` (fast start, long float); hold and hover
  1-1.2 s with a 1-2 % vertical bob, pairs bobbing in opposite directions;
  return 0.5-0.6 s `ease_in_expo` so all arrive on the same frame.
- Snap overshoot on the centre: 1.08 in 2 frames, 0.99, settle by +0.55 s.
  Without it the judge called the merge "stiff, anti-climactic".
- Clones step down in size outward (1.0 / 0.9 / 0.8) and spacing about one
  product width (±0.20, ±0.40 for a product 0.2 of the frame wide).
- End on the merged product, not on the empty backdrop: trim the backdrop
  to the render's end.

## Sound for it

Soft airy swishes panned with the clones as they leave; nothing during the
hover but music. The snap is the cue: a click that sits *above* the music
(`hit_transient` plus a bright synth `mouse_click`, both -6 dB) with a low
thud under it. The recorded-kit `mouse_click` came back as a soft UI hover
sound; use `kit: "synth"` for a click with bite.

## Common mistakes

- Cubic ease on the fan-out: reads as linear and robotic (judge, pass 1).
- Clones arriving at different frames: the snap goes mushy.
- Centre clone not on the top track: the others land over it.
- A dry click with no low end.
