# T003 Locked-axis 3D model-year morph

## What it is

One locked profile camera on a car. The wheels and ground shadow never move
while the body changes from one model year to the next; a small generation
label flips with it. A heritage or model-line film is a chain of these
morphs (eight chapters in 90 s, 8-15 s apart), with no cuts at all.

## Build it in Zenvi

Zenvi cannot interpolate one mesh into another. What holds the axis is that
`model_showcase` with the same params renders the same camera path for every
model: render each generation with identical params, then overlap the two
renders *at the same render time*, so their cameras match frame for frame,
and dissolve. The models must share wheel positions and bounding box (same
length, width and height, or the framing rescales); a 2 mm triangle at the
tallest model's roof corner pads a lower model's box.

```json
add_3d_scene_tool {"scene": "model_showcase", "quality": "draft", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 4, "track": "1", "params": {"model": "<gen1.obj>", "move": "turntable", "turn_degrees": 0, "stage": "studio", "material": "matte", "color": "#2b2f36", "elevation": 4, "background": "#d9dbde", "shadow": true, "seed": 7}}
add_3d_scene_tool {"scene": "model_showcase", "quality": "draft", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 4, "track": "2", "params": {"model": "<gen2.obj>", "move": "turntable", "turn_degrees": 0, "stage": "studio", "material": "matte", "color": "#2b2f36", "elevation": 4, "background": "#d9dbde", "shadow": true, "seed": 7}}
trim_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<gen1>", "edge": "right", "at_seconds": 2.4}
trim_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<gen2>", "edge": "left", "at_seconds": 1.8}
fade_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<gen2>", "fade_in_seconds": 0.6}
```

Drag edges with `edge`/`at_seconds` (not new in/out points): the clips keep
their place in render time, which is what keeps the camera locked. The
turntable still pushes in by about 5 %; that is fine because both renders
share it. A profile view needs the model turned to face the camera: a model
built along X needed a -24° yaw before import in this test.

Generation label, small mono, top left (3 % height):

```json
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "text_scramble", "position_seconds": 0.2, "duration_seconds": 1.6, "placement": "over_video", "params": {"text": "GEN 1 · 1994", "label": "MODEL LINE · 01", "size": 34, "align": "left", "palette": "light", "ink": "#1d2026", "exit": true}}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "text_scramble", "position_seconds": 2.3, "duration_seconds": 1.7, "placement": "over_video", "params": {"text": "GEN 2 · 2026", "label": "MODEL LINE · 02", "size": 34, "align": "left", "palette": "light", "ink": "#1d2026"}}
set_clip_properties_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<both labels>"], "properties": {"location_y": -0.36}}
```

A push on top must be one straight line across both clips (`interpolation:
"linear"`, gen2's first value = gen1's value at 1.8 s), or the bodies jump:

```json
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<gen1>", "property": "scale_x", "interpolation": "linear", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0.0333, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.4, "value": 1.0296}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<gen2>", "property": "scale_x", "interpolation": "linear", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 1.8, "value": 1.0222}, {"timeline_seconds": 3.96, "value": 1.0495}]}
```

(and the same on `scale_y`). Fallback with footage: two locked-off profile
shots of the real cars, `align_match_cut_tool` on the wheel hubs
(`fit: "position_scale"`), then the same 0.6 s dissolve. An AI morph
(`generate_transition_clip_tool`) is desktop-only; on the CLI and web there
is no real shape interpolation.

## Timing & taste rules

- Morph 0.5-0.7 s; each generation holds at least 1.5 s before the next
  (references: one every 8-15 s on a long film).
- Wheels, ground line and camera do not move by a pixel across the morph:
  check frames 0.1 s before, during and after.
- Label 2.4-3.2 % height, out before the morph, the new one in just after.
- Keep the grade neutral: a vignette of 0.4 on a light studio floor and
  up-scaling the label block both made the second pass look cheap.

## Sound for it

One whoosh per morph centred on it (-12 dB), a reverse swell into it, a
light transient as the new body settles, a tap on each label flip
(-20 dB), room tone under everything and a bloom tail at the end. The
reference film does exactly one whoosh per morph and nothing else.

## Common mistakes

- Renders with different params or durations: the cameras no longer match.
- Models of different length or height: the framing rescales and the wheels
  jump.
- Trimming with new in-points instead of dragging the edge.
- Grading or vignetting hard to "add depth" to clay renders.
