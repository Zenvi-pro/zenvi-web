# T023 One-hue treatment with glow silhouette

## What it is

One grade for the whole piece: a single hue family (cobalt, amber, violet)
or zero saturation, with the focal figure the brightest thing in frame. The
figure becomes a flat silhouette with an inner fill and a 30-50 px outer
glow on a crushed field. It shines in lyric videos and in the "feature
stage" beats of earbud and audio product films (each feature its own hue).

## Build it in Zenvi

Source: a person on a dark or plain backdrop, full body, moving (a dancer on
black matted cleanly in the test). `subject_glow_tool` mattes the person,
puts the filled, glowing silhouette on a track above and dims the footage.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<dancer on black>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 0.5, "end_seconds": 4.0, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<same dancer, other phrase>", "position_seconds": 3.5, "start_seconds": 9.0, "end_seconds": 12.5, "track": "1"}
subject_glow_tool {"clip_id": "<shot 1>", "look": "glow", "color": "#cfe4ff", "glow_color": "#2f6bff", "glow": 0.9, "background": "dim"}
subject_glow_tool {"clip_id": "<shot 2>", "look": "glow", "color": "#cfe4ff", "glow_color": "#2f6bff", "glow": 0.9, "background": "dim"}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<shot 1>", "<shot 2>"], "saturation": 0.9, "contrast": 0.2, "shadows": -0.3, "temperature": -0.6, "tint": -0.1}
```

Fill and glow are the same hue, fill lighter (here pale blue #cfe4ff, glow
cobalt #2f6bff): two hues in fill and glow break the one-hue rule. For the
product-film variant (black figure on a saturated field) use
`"color": "#05070d"`, `"background": "black"` and put a one-hue
`gradient_field` motion block on the track below. Every shot in the piece
gets the same call; a second hue arrives only with a new chapter.

```json
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<pulse track>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 5, "duration_seconds": 7.5, "track": "5"}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "hit_transient", "at_seconds": 3.5, "gain_db": -14}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "tail_bloom", "at_seconds": 3.5, "gain_db": -16}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

`subject_glow_tool` is slow on 4K stock (7.5 min for a 3.5 s clip here):
use 1080p sources or keep shots short.

## Timing & taste rules

- One hue (or none) for the whole piece; the figure's fill is the
  brightest value in frame, the field under 10 % luma.
- Glow 0.8-0.9; less reads as a cut-out, not light.
- Shots 2-4 s; cut on a strong pose or extension and change scale between
  shots (wide to medium), or the piece feels like one long VFX test (the
  judge's second fix).
- End on a held final pose, not mid-move.

## Sound for it

The look is static in colour, so rhythm has to come from the music: a track
with a clear pulse, with hits on the dancer's extensions. A low drone alone
was scored 2/10 for sound. A soft transient plus bloom tail on each cut,
and a reverb tail at the end instead of a hard stop.

## Common mistakes

- A different grade per shot; or a coloured glow over a differently
  coloured fill.
- Busy backdrops: the matte edge flickers and the field is no longer crushed.
- A drone bed with no pulse; ending mid-motion.
