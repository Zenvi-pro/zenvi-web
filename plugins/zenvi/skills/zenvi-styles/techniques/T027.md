# T027 Angled polygon matte on black

## What it is

Live action seen only through an angled wedge, trapezoid or chevron cut out
of pure black, like light thrown from a screen. A subject crosses the edge and
the inside switches shot, or the whole wedge turns 90° in one beat. Laptop and
device launch films, product CGI openers.

## Build it in Zenvi

There is no polygon mask with keyframed vertices. Build the wedge from two
black occluder planes above the footage instead: each is a black card blown
up 3x, slid so one edge sits on the wedge's apex, pivoted on that edge and
rotated. Two edges through one point make a V; keyframing both rotations
opens, narrows or turns it. Generate the card once:
`ffmpeg -f lavfi -i color=black:s=1920x1080 -frames:v 1 black.png`.

```json
import_files_tool {"paths": ["/abs/path/black.png"]}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<walk A>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 0.5, "end_seconds": 3.1, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<walk B>", "position_seconds": 2.6, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 2.65, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<laptop>", "position_seconds": 4.25, "start_seconds": 3.0, "end_seconds": 5.48, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<black.png>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 0, "end_seconds": 6.75, "track": "2"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<black.png>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 0, "end_seconds": 6.75, "track": "3"}
set_clip_properties_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<left occluder>"], "properties": {"scale_x": 3, "scale_y": 3, "location_x": -1.5, "origin_x": 1.0, "origin_y": 0.5}}
set_clip_properties_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<right occluder>"], "properties": {"scale_x": 3, "scale_y": 3, "location_x": 1.5, "origin_x": 0.001, "origin_y": 0.5}}
```

`location_x` is in frame widths, so ±1.5 puts each 3x card's inner edge on
the centre line. `origin_y` sets the apex height: `0.5 + (apex_y - 0.5) / 3`.
Use `origin_x: 0.001`, not 0: a 0 renders as the default 0.5 and the right
card pivots on its middle, leaving a corner in frame.

Open the wedge (downward V from the centre, 64° wide), hold, then turn it to
point right in one beat. Positive rotation is clockwise:

```json
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<left occluder>", "property": "rotation", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 0}, {"timeline_seconds": 0.5, "value": 32, "ease": "ease_out_cubic"}, {"timeline_seconds": 4.0, "value": 32}, {"timeline_seconds": 4.5, "value": -58, "ease": "ease_in_out_cubic"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<right occluder>", "property": "rotation", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 0}, {"timeline_seconds": 0.5, "value": -32, "ease": "ease_out_cubic"}, {"timeline_seconds": 4.0, "value": -32}, {"timeline_seconds": 4.5, "value": -122, "ease": "ease_in_out_cubic"}]}
set_clip_properties_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<laptop>"], "properties": {"location_x": 0.22, "location_y": 0.12}}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<walk A>", "<walk B>", "<laptop>"], "contrast": 0.15}
trim_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<laptop>", "end_seconds": 5.48}
```

Slide each shot (`location_x/y`) so its subject sits inside the wedge, and
end every footage clip with the occluders: footage that outlives them shows
full frame. Cut the shot change inside the wedge where the subject crosses
the edge, and cut the third shot in mid-turn (here 4.25 s) so the move hides
it.

Fallback without layers to spare: a bundled triangle or losange luma wipe
(`update_transition_tool` `mask` "big losange", or `mask_file_id` with your
own wedge PNG) gives a fixed polygon reveal between two clips, with no turn.

## Timing & taste rules

- Wedge opens in 0.4-0.5 s from closed (both rotations 0) to 60-70° wide.
- Hold each shot inside it 1.5-2.6 s; the turn is 90° in 0.5 s
  (`ease_in_out_cubic`). At 0.4 s with expo easing the judge read it as a
  one-frame glitch; at 0.5 s cubic it read as stiff. There is no motion
  blur on transforms, so the whoosh carries the move.
- Use bright footage that fills the frame. On dark stage footage the wedge
  edges vanish into the black and the shape never reads (first attempt).
- Put the apex at the frame centre when the wedge will turn: turning about an
  apex near an edge swings the wedge out of frame.
- The last shot needs something to look at: a blank white laptop screen was
  flagged twice as a dead end. End on a close to black or a packshot (T089).

## Sound for it

A short whoosh plus a `sub_drop` (-12 dB) on the opening, `hit_transient`
(-10 dB) on the shot switch, `whoosh_rise` peaking mid-turn and
`impact_boom` pitch -4 with `tail_bloom` on the turn landing. Under it a low
drone; nothing melodic until the wedge settles.

## Common mistakes

- `origin_x: 0` on the right card (renders as 0.5).
- Occluders shorter than the footage: a frame of unmasked picture at the end.
- Dark footage, so the matte is invisible.
- A wedge that turns about an apex near the bottom and leaves the frame.
