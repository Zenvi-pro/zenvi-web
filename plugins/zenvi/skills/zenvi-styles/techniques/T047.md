# T047 Transcript-keyed footage cutaway

## What it is

While a host or narrator keeps talking, the picture cuts to a clip that
proves the word just spoken ("machined" shows the milling machine), for
1.5-4.6 s (1.9-2.9 s per claim), then back to the A-roll. A label pill can
open on the same frame. Brand stories, reviews, tours, explainers.

## Build it in Zenvi

Get word times, pick the proof word of each claim, and put an insert on
the track above the A-roll starting on that word's onset.

```json
transcribe_media_tool {"file_id": "<voice or A-roll>"}
get_transcript_tool {"file_id": "<voice or A-roll>"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<A-roll>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 0, "end_seconds": 8.6, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<milling machine>", "position_seconds": 0.3, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 2.6, "track": "2"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<rain on glass>", "position_seconds": 2.8, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 4.6, "track": "2"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<heat-haze road>", "position_seconds": 4.8, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 6.6, "track": "2"}
set_clip_audio_video_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<inserts>"], "audio": "off"}
set_track_role_tool {"track": "5", "role": "voice"}
automate_volume_tool {"track": "3", "shape": "dip", "start_seconds": 0.2, "end_seconds": 8.4, "db": -8}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

`position_seconds` of each insert is the proof word's start time from
`get_transcript_tool`. There is no one-call "place at word" with a miss
report (`word-anchored-placement`) and no automatic noun-onset insert
picker (`transcript-broll-inserter`): read the times and place by hand.
`search_clip_scenes_tool` / `slice_clip_at_best_match_tool` find the proof
moment inside long footage.

## Timing & taste rules

- Insert starts on the word onset, within 2 frames; never before it.
- 1.5-4.6 s per insert; 1.9-2.9 s for one claim.
- Return to the A-roll at least every second claim, and for the last line.
- Inserts muted; the voice never drops under them.
- The insert must show the literal noun or verb, not a mood shot.

## Sound for it

The voice is the lead; the bed sits 8 dB under it (dip or
`duck_under_speech_tool`). One quiet foley cue from the insert (rain, the
mill) at -20 dB can sell it; no whooshes on these cuts.

## Common mistakes

- Cutting on the sentence start, a second before the proof word.
- Generic b-roll that does not show the word.
- Inserts so long the speaker disappears.
- Insert sound left on, fighting the voice.
