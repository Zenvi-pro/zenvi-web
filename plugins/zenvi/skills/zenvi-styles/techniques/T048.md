# T048 Verb card then proof cut

## What it is

A one-word card (CODE, TRAIN, EDIT) on black for one beat, hard cut to a
1.5-2 s picture that proves the word, three units back to back. The last
proof ends on a fast ease-out zoom-out that reveals the whole thing. The
middle of a laptop, workstation or software launch film, where the
features are verbs.

## Build it in Zenvi

Put the music on first and cut on its grid: `detect_beats_tool` on the
music file, then trim the music so a downbeat lands at 0. At 124.8 BPM one
beat is 0.481 s: card = 1 beat, proof = 4 beats, unit = 2.405 s.

`trim_clip_tool` keeps a block's position and moves its in-point, so trim
the blocks to start mid-action: the proof must already be happening on its
first frame (code half typed, the line already drawing). Set `exit: false`
on blocks you trim, or their fade-out lands in the proof as a dark frame.
With real screen recordings, use them for all three proofs instead of
blocks; that is the faithful version.

## Timing & taste rules

- Card 0.4-0.5 s (one beat, 12-15 frames), proof 1.5-2.5 s, exactly three
  units. Every cut on a beat; the card is black with one word only.
- Verb at the largest `text_size` (0.3). At 0.28 it reads at about 20 %
  of frame height, smaller than the reference's full-bleed words.
- `code_card` draws a window about a third of the frame wide with short
  code: give it 8+ lines, or the proof reads as an empty editor.
- The zoom-out is 2.2x to 1.0 in 1.2 s, `ease_out_expo`: fast start, long
  settle. Linear reads as a slow drift.

## Sound for it

Each card gets a low punch plus a transient on its first frame
(`impact_punch` -9 dB, `hit_transient` -12 dB). Proofs get the foley of the
verb: `typing_burst` under the code, a short `riser_tonal` under the chart,
`whoosh_fall` plus `sub_drop` on the zoom-out. Music runs under at full
level; master with `master_audio_tool` `preset: "social"`.

## Common mistakes

- A proof that starts on an empty state (blank editor, empty axes).
- Cards held longer than a beat: the rhythm turns into a slideshow.
- Four or more units, or a different card style per unit.
- A proof that does not show the word (TRAIN over a stock office shot).
