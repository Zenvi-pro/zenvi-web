# T057 Glass UI cards over nature plate

## What it is

Frosted glass cards (real backdrop blur, soft shadow) float over a
photoreal landscape (misty hills, meadow, sky) while the frame drifts in;
a pointer clicks one card's toggle and it changes state. The unexpected
calm sets it apart from dark dashboards. 3-6 s per use, one or two per reel.

## Build it in Zenvi

The plate goes into the block as `background` so the glass blurs it.

```json
gather_stock_footage_tool {"queries": ["green rolling hills misty morning"], "count": 2, "orientation": "landscape", "min_seconds": 5}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "glass_cards", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 5, "track": "1", "placement": "full_frame",
  "params": {"background": "<plate file id>", "headline": "Every tool, *one* calm place", "cards": ["Calendar", "Billing", "Deploys"],
             "details": ["3 today", "Paid", "Paused"], "click": 2, "status": "Live", "palette": "light", "accent": "#1F5F4A", "exit": false}}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<glass block>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"seconds": 5, "value": 1.06}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<glass block>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"seconds": 5, "value": 1.06}]}
add_vignette_tool {"clip_ids": ["<glass block>"], "amount": 0.25, "feather": 0.7}
```

For a prompt instead of cards, `liquid_glass` with the same `background`.
Then cut to the next state (a counter, the end card) within 1-1.5 s of the
toggle.

## Timing & taste rules

- Block 4-5 s: cards in by 1.5 s, click at about 55 % of the block, then
  at most 1-1.5 s of hold. At 5.8 s the judge called the end a stall.
- Plate pale and soft: lots of sky or mist, low saturation. A saturated
  green plate fights the glass; grade it lighter or pick mistier stock.
- Push 1.0 to 1.06 over the block, linear; vignette 0.2-0.3 only.
- One accent, a calm deep colour (`#1F5F4A`, `#2F6BFF`), never red.

## Sound for it

`swish_airy` (-14 dB) as the cards rise, a quiet `ui_tap` (-16 dB) per
card landing, `mouse_click` on the click frame and `toggle` right after.
Minimal tech music with a clear pulse at full level.

## Common mistakes

- Holding the finished state 2 s or more.
- White type straight on the photo outside the glass.
- A busy, high-contrast plate (city, crowds) under the glass.

## Verified

2026-10-28. Pexels misty green hills as the block's `background`, 5.8 s
at 1920x1080: `~/zenvi-motion/fleet/artifacts/sk2-product-brand/techniques/T057.*`.
Judge 6.36/10 (one pass): "glass UI elements blend smoothly into the
scenic nature plate". Its fixes were the pointer path's easing and the
card stagger (inside the block, no parameter for either) and the stall
after the toggle (block shortened to 5 s in the recipe above). The plate
was greener and darker than the reference's pale ones. No cast shadow
on the ground, no real depth of field.
