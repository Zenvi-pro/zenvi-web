# T078 Close-mic transient on detail insert

## What it is

A short insert of a mechanism (button, key, latch, lever, handle) gets one
dry, exaggerated sound, recorded as if the mic were touching it, on frame 0
or within 1 s of the cut, at or above the music. It makes a picture of a
part feel engineered. Car interiors, gadgets, watches, doors in a house tour.

## Build it in Zenvi

Mute the stock clips' own sound, find the contact frame in each insert by
eye (`watch_clip_window_tool` or a contact sheet), then pick a real
recording per action from the kit:

```json

search_sound_library_tool {"category": "click", "query": "metal", "count": 4}
search_sound_library_tool {"category": "thud", "query": "metal", "count": 4}
set_clip_audio_video_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<all picture clips>"], "audio": "off"}
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<steady beat>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 30, "duration_seconds": 10.4, "track": "3"}
add_sfx_tool {"provider": "kit", "sound_id": "42943", "at_seconds": 2.45, "gain_db": -4}
add_sfx_tool {"provider": "kit", "sound_id": "164641", "at_seconds": 3.75, "gain_db": -3}
add_sfx_tool {"provider": "kit", "sound_id": "96472", "at_seconds": 4.55, "gain_db": -5}

```

Kit ids are the bare numbers (`"42943"`, not `"kit:42943"`: the prefix is
refused). Here: a large push-button switch on the console press, a pen
click on the key turn, a short wooden door shut on the gear lever.

## Timing & taste rules

- The sound's transient on the contact frame, not the cut, when they
  differ; never more than 1 s after the cut.
- Gain -3 to -5 dB on the clip, so after mastering it sits 0-3 dB over the
  bed: these cues are meant to be heard over the music.
- Only 20-60 % of cuts get one. Inserts under 0.5 s stay dry.
- One sound per action; a different recording for each (the kit's variety
  rule does this when you pass `near_seconds` to the search).
- Dry: no reverb tail on the transient; the bed carries the space.

## Sound for it

A steady bed (a beat or a pulse) under everything is required: with a
sparse drum bed the judge heard "isolated clicks over silence" (3.3).
With a steady beat the same cues "sell the tactile theme and cut through
the mix" (4.6). Layer a quiet actuator hum or tumbler under a key turn
when the action is slow.

## Common mistakes

- No bed: the transients sound sterile, not premium.
- The cue on the cut when the hand touches 10 frames later (judge: "the
  hand motion is sluggish compared to the click"); slide the sound, or
  speed the insert up 120-150 % so contact meets the click.
- A whoosh or impact recipe instead of a mechanical recording.
- Every insert clicking; the pattern stops reading as real.
