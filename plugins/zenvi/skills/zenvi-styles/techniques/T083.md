# T083 Low-pass muffle then open

## What it is

The sound plays as if from behind something (headphones not yet on, a
closed lid, a mode off): low-passed and a few dB down. When the cause is
removed, the filter opens and the level steps up. It proves noise
cancelling, a speaker, a mode, or just opens a scene.

## Build it in Zenvi

Split the bed into two clips at the moment the cause goes: the first on
its own track with the muffle EQ, the second continuing from the same
source time, unfiltered.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<headphones on table>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 3.5, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<wearing them>", "position_seconds": 2.5, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 6.0, "track": "1"}
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<bed>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 30, "duration_seconds": 2.5, "track": "3"}
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<bed>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 2.5, "start_seconds": 32.5, "duration_seconds": 3.5, "track": "4"}
eq_tool {"track": "3", "preset": "muffle"}
automate_volume_tool {"track": "3", "shape": "dip", "start_seconds": 0, "end_seconds": 2.5, "db": -5, "fade_ms": 0}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "cloth", "at_seconds": 2.45, "gain_db": -14}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

`muffle` is a low-pass at 800 Hz. For a slow open (a lid lifting), give
`eq_tool` `bands` on each of 2-3 short clips with rising cut-off instead.
Note the desktop app hears these EQ bands at other frequencies until its
Parametric EQ fix lands; the CLI and cloud renders are correct.

## Timing & taste rules

- Muffled at 600-1000 Hz and 4-6 dB down; open on the frame the cause
  goes, with no crossfade longer than 2 frames.
- Muffle 2-4 s: long enough to notice, short of annoying.
- The open lands on a hard cut or a visible action (on, off, lid up).

## Sound for it

The bed is the effect. A soft cloth or click on the action; no riser. The
open is the loudest moment so far.

## Common mistakes

- A slow fade instead of an open: it reads as a volume ride.
- Muffling everything, voice included, when only the music is "behind".
- The open half a second before or after the action.
