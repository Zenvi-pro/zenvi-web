# T084 Music stops dead, then hit or line

## What it is

The bed ends on a hard cut with no fade, and the film plays dry: one line
of dialogue, a deadpan scene, room tone. Then the music returns on a later
hard cut with a hit (a brass stab, a drum). The silence makes the line or
the joke land. Car spots and fashion films, once per film.

## Build it in Zenvi

Cut the bed into two clips rather than automating it down: the first ends
exactly on the cut, the second starts on the return cut, from a downbeat.

```json
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<bed>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 30, "duration_seconds": 2.4, "track": "3"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<line or dialogue>", "position_seconds": 2.9, "start_seconds": 0, "end_seconds": 2.0, "track": "5"}
set_track_role_tool {"track": "5", "role": "voice"}
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<bed>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 5.0, "start_seconds": 44, "duration_seconds": 4.4, "track": "4"}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "hit_transient", "at_seconds": 5.0, "gain_db": -8}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "impact_boom", "at_seconds": 5.0, "gain_db": -5}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

On a single bed clip the same stop is `automate_volume_tool` with
`"shape": "points"`, `"curve": "constant"`, `"mode": "absolute"` to -60 dB
from the stop to the return.

## Timing & taste rules

- Stop with no fade (a 1-frame cut at most), on the picture cut.
- Silence 2-4 s in a short piece; up to about 14 s for a scene in a 60 s
  film. Leave 0.3-0.5 s of quiet before the line starts.
- Return on a hard cut and a downbeat of the bed, with a hit on it.
- Keep something on screen during the silence: a lit detail or a face.
  A near-black frame here read as "broken footage".

## Sound for it

Dry dialogue or room tone only during the stop; no riser, no reverb tail
from the bed. The return is the loudest moment after the stop: transient
plus boom, the bed at full level from its first frame.

## Common mistakes

- Fading the bed out instead of stopping it dead.
- Black or empty picture under the silence.
- Returning the music mid-phrase instead of on a downbeat.
- Using it twice in one film.
