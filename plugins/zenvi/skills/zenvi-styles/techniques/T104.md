# T104 Shape-registered match cut

## What it is

Shots whose main shape matches (wheel rim, headlight, gauge, steering
wheel; pupil, sun, can rim) cut one beat apart (0.3-0.5 s), each aligned so
the shape sits at the same centre and size. The eye stays still while the
world changes. Car films, science and travel montages.

## Build it in Zenvi

Gather round subjects, then let the montage tool detect, align and cut:

```json
gather_stock_footage_tool {"queries": ["car wheel rim close up", "car headlight close up", "speedometer gauge close up", "steering wheel close up", "car tire spinning close up"], "count": 5, "orientation": "landscape", "min_seconds": 4}
import_files_tool {"paths": ["<bed.mp3>"]}
detect_subject_tool {"file_id": "<each file>"}
match_cut_montage_tool {"file_ids": ["<rim>", "<headlight>", "<gauge>", "<steering wheel>", "<tyre>"], "music_file_id": "<bed>", "duration_seconds": 7, "min_frames": 9, "max_frames": 15, "flash": false, "transitions": "none"}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

Check `detect_subject_tool` boxes first and pass corrected boxes as
`subjects` where it picked the wrong circle. For cuts you lay yourself,
`align_match_cut_tool` with each clip's box does the registration.
Non-round shapes (a pose, an old and a new part) need hand-set boxes; a
guide graphic over the shape needs `subject-registered-graphic-overlay`.

## Timing & taste rules

- 9-15 frames per shot (0.3-0.5 s) in a car film; 3-8 only for a flash
  montage.
- Hold the first and last shot 1-2 s.
- Same centre within 2 % of the frame, same size within 10 %.
- No white flashes in a premium car film (`flash: false`), hard cuts.
- 4-8 subjects; a subject may return once.

## Sound for it

The cuts sit on the beat; the drum carries them, no SFX per cut. One hit
on the landing shot.

## Common mistakes

- Mismatched sizes (`cover: "zoom_all"` keeps them equal).
- Shapes found in the wrong place (a hubcap logo instead of the rim).
- Too many flashes and punches for the genre.
