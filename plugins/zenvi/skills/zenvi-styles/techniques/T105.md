# T105 Value-inversion jump cut

## What it is

A hard cut from a frame that is about 95 % black (a rim, a badge edge) to a
frame that is about 90 % white (the car on a white cyclorama), on a drum
hit, with no flash overlay. The jump in value is the flash. It resets
attention and moves a film from its low-key half to its high-key half.

## Build it in Zenvi

The dark side can be made from any shot by cropping tight and crushing; the
white side must be shot high-key (a white studio or bright overcast).
Lifting a normal daylight shot washes it out (see Verified).

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<car>", "position_seconds": 2.4, "start_seconds": 0.2, "end_seconds": 3.0, "track": "1"}
set_clip_properties_tool {"timeline_clip_ids": ["<dark>"], "properties": {"scale_x": 2.4, "scale_y": 2.4, "location_x": 0.55, "location_y": -0.42}}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<dark>", "exposure": -1.4, "shadows": -1.0, "contrast": 0.4, "saturation": 0}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<same car, white studio>", "position_seconds": 3.4, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 5.4, "track": "1"}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<white>", "exposure": 0.2, "contrast": 0.15, "saturation": 0.6}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<white>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 3.4, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 7.4, "value": 1.04}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<white>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 3.4, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 7.4, "value": 1.04}]}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "hit_transient", "at_seconds": 3.4, "gain_db": -8}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "impact_boom", "at_seconds": 3.4, "gain_db": -5}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

Put the cut on a drum hit of the bed (`detect_beats_tool`, then
`move_clips_tool`), not the other way round.

## Timing & taste rules

- Dark frame 0.5-1.0 s, with a visible rim highlight; 2.6 s of black read
  as "the video crashed" (the judge's first fix).
- White frame keeps contrast: deep tyres and glass, white background. Not a
  lifted, desaturated daylight shot (exposure +0.55, saturation 0.15 read
  as "washed-out, flat").
- No flash, no dissolve, no whoosh tail: one transient plus one low boom
  on the cut frame.
- Same car on both sides. Hold the white frame 2-4 s.

## Sound for it

The drum hit is the cut: a transient and a low boom on the frame, the bed
continuing or re-entering on it. A short riser into the cut is optional;
keep it under the bed.

## Common mistakes

- Black held too long, with nothing in it.
- Faking high-key by lifting exposure on an ordinary shot.
- Adding a white flash on top (the reference uses none).
- A different car or angle family on the white side.

## Verified

2026-10-28. Pexels white coupe in daylight (front wheel cropped 2.4x and
crushed for the dark side, the full shot lifted for the white side), 9.4 s
at 1920x1080, shared with T084: render, contact sheet and judge in
`~/zenvi-motion/fleet/artifacts/sk2-product-brand/techniques/T105.*`.
One pass, 3.4/10: the dark side held 2.6 s and read as dead air, and the
lifted daylight shot read as washed out, not high-key. The rules above
(0.5-1 s dark, real white-studio footage with contrast kept) are the
fixes; not re-rendered (budget). No missing tool; the limit is stock that
has a true white-cyc shot of the same car.
