# T106 Warp-tunnel speed rush

## What it is

A 1-1.5 s CG star-streak tunnel (purple, cyan, white) fired between two
sections, ending in a white flash that hard-cuts onto a physical action:
a cable slamming into a port, a key pressed, a lid closed. A launch film's
gear change, once or twice per film.

## Build it in Zenvi

The `hyperspace` block is the tunnel; it runs 1.5 s at least and ends on
its own flash, so render 1.6 s and trim the slow ring-draw off the front.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<device shot with a screen>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 3, "end_seconds": 5.4, "track": "1"}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<device shot>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.4, "value": 1.2, "ease": "ease_in_cubic"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<device shot>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.4, "value": 1.2, "ease": "ease_in_cubic"}]}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "hyperspace", "position_seconds": 2.4, "duration_seconds": 1.6, "track": "1", "placement": "full_frame",
  "params": {"palette": "night", "colors": ["#9B7BFF", "#4FD8FF", "#FFFFFF"], "density": 500, "flash": "white"}}
trim_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<hyperspace>", "start_seconds": 0.4, "end_seconds": 1.6}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<hand plugs the cable>", "position_seconds": 3.6, "start_seconds": 2.2, "end_seconds": 4.6, "track": "1"}
apply_motion_transition_tool {"preset": "zoom_through", "clip1_id": "<device shot>", "clip2_id": "<hyperspace>", "focus_x": 0.68, "focus_y": 0.35}
```

The cut out of the flash is a hard cut, no transition: the flash is the
transition. Trim the action clip so the contact (plug seats, key bottoms
out) lands 4-8 frames after the cut, not a second later.

## Timing & taste rules

- Tunnel on screen 1-1.5 s. The ring-and-glint start of the block is slow;
  trim 0.3-0.4 s off its head so streaks are already pouring.
- Push the outgoing shot into the screen 1.0 to 1.2 with `ease_in_cubic`
  before `zoom_through` (focus on the screen): at 1.08 linear it reads as
  a static shot and a jump cut (the judge's first fix).
- The incoming action shot must be lit and graded close to the tunnel's
  colour temperature; a warm daylight desk after a blue tunnel reads as a
  mismatch.
- At most twice per film; never on a calm brand piece.

## Sound for it

A `whoosh_rise` (1.2-1.6 s, -8 dB) ending on the flash frame, a short
whoosh on the zoom into the screen, then on the hard cut `impact_slam`
(-7 dB) with `sub_drop` (-12 dB), and a dry `mouse_click` or click foley
on the contact frame. The whoosh sits above the music.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving the block's slow opening in: the rush starts with dead frames.
- Cutting to a shot where nothing physical happens.
- A soft fade out of the tunnel instead of the flash plus hard cut.
- No click on the contact: the action needs its own transient.
