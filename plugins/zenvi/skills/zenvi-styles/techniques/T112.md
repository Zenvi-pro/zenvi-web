# T112 Buy-or-skip verdict interstitial

## What it is

A full-screen colour card in the middle of a review: BUY lands in huge
caps (about 22 % of frame height), holds a beat, then morphs into SKIP
with a bounce and scribble marks, about 2.7 s, then back to the footage.
It marks the "should you buy it" pivot, about halfway through a review.

## Build it in Zenvi

`type_morph` does the BUY to SKIP morph; put it full frame on a flat
colour (`paper`). `particle_burst` with `shape: "lines"` gives the
hand-drawn burst marks on the morph.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<review footage>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 2, "end_seconds": 3.443, "track": "1"}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "type_morph", "position_seconds": 1.443, "duration_seconds": 2.9, "track": "2", "placement": "full_frame",
  "params": {"words": ["BUY", "SKIP"], "beat": 1.2, "morph": 0.45, "sweep": 0.3, "weight": 900, "color": "ink", "max_size": 340, "paper": "#F4C430", "palette": "light", "exit": false}}
trim_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<type_morph>", "start_seconds": 0.2, "end_seconds": 2.9}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "particle_burst", "position_seconds": 2.2, "duration_seconds": 1.2, "track": "3",
  "params": {"shape": "lines", "count": 18, "rings": false, "colors": ["#111111"], "origin": [{"x": 0.5, "y": 0.5}]}}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<review footage>", "position_seconds": 4.143, "start_seconds": 6, "end_seconds": 7.2, "track": "1"}
```

Fallback for the faithful look (paper-cut letters with 2-frame stop-motion
jitter and orbiting doodle question marks): no tool; `type_morph` gives
clean vector letters only.

## Timing & taste rules

- 2.5-2.9 s on screen: BUY 1.0-1.2 s, morph 0.45 s, SKIP held 1 s.
- `max_size` 340 at 1080 gives caps about 22 % of frame height; the
  verdict is at least 4x the review's spec-card type.
- `type_morph` grows the first word out of dots: trim its first 0.2 s so
  BUY is already landing on frame one (the judge flagged the empty yellow
  frames).
- One loud flat colour (yellow, red) with black ink; no footage behind.
- The burst lines are black and thin; white sparks read as a cheap flare.

## Sound for it

Whoosh on the cut in (-9 dB), `impact_punch` as BUY lands, a second
whoosh into the morph and `impact_slam` (-8 dB) as SKIP lands, a short
whoosh on the cut back. Music keeps going under it.

## Common mistakes

- Dead flat-colour frames before the word.
- Easing the morph slowly: the joke needs snap.
- Both words the same weight as the review's lower thirds.
- Using it more than once per review.
