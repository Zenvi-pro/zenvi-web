# T116 Giant-word kinetic statement

## What it is

One hero word set 3-9x the size of the small words around it ("built
for" small, AGENTS huge), popping in with an ease-out. The climax variant
holds a prefix and swaps the tail word every 8-12 frames ("Edit like / a
pro / a studio / anyone"). The headline beat of a SaaS launch reel.

## Build it in Zenvi

`add_kinetic_title_tool` `style: "creator"` is the size contrast: small
connectors, the emphasis word in huge condensed caps (about 8x at
`text_size` 0.05). `word_rotator` is the swap.

```json
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "gradient_field", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 5.8, "track": "1", "params": {"style": "aurora", "palette": "light", "grain": true, "exit": false}}
add_kinetic_title_tool {"text": "Built for AGENTS.", "position_seconds": 0.2, "duration_seconds": 2.2, "style": "creator", "animation": "pop", "emphasis": ["AGENTS."], "accent": "#2F6BFF", "ink": "#111111", "text_size": 0.05, "y": 0.5}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "word_rotator", "position_seconds": 2.4, "duration_seconds": 2.4, "track": "3",
  "params": {"prefix": "Edit like", "words": ["a pro", "a studio", "a team", "a robot", "a crew", "anyone"], "style": "flip", "palette": "light", "accent": "#2F6BFF", "exit": false}}
```

`word_rotator` spreads the words over the block's length: with 6 words
over 2.4 s each swap is about 12 frames; shorten the block or add words
to get 8 frames. Then a hard stop on the last word with a hit, or cut to
the end card.

## Timing & taste rules

- Hero word 25-45 % of frame height, connectors 4-7 %: ratio 3-9x.
- Set `y: 0.5`: the creator style's default spot sat low in frame on a
  plain field (the judge's first fix).
- Swaps every 8-12 frames (0.27-0.4 s); at 0.6 s per word it reads as
  sluggish. Prefix never moves; only the tail changes.
- `word_rotator` sets its line at a calm 5-6 % of height in this test
  (`size` 150 did not make it bigger): fine for the swap, but the swap is
  not the giant word; keep the size contrast on the first beat.
- One accent colour, light field, no photo behind the type.

## Sound for it

`pop` plus a soft `impact_punch` on the hero word's landing, a
`ui_tap` (-14 dB) on every swap, an `impact_punch` on the last word.
Music with a clear pulse; land swaps on its 8th notes.

## Common mistakes

- Every line the same size: no hero.
- A swap that drifts or ghosts between words; use `style: "flip"`.
- No ending: land the last word on a hit and hold 1 s, or cut to the logo.
