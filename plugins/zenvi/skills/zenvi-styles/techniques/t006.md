# t006 Refraction rings that follow the body

## What it is

Glass shockwave rings with rainbow dispersion expand from a dancer's hand,
hip or head, one per beat, 2-3 s each: the picture of sound for headphones
and speakers. The reference films open on it. Dancer on black or a single
hue (t023), product absent or small.

## Build it in Zenvi

Zenvi has no dispersive glass ring and no body tracker on this surface. The
honest version: `particle_burst` in `rings` mode, one block per beat, its
`origin` read off a frame at the burst time, then the dancer cut out on top
so the rings pass *behind* her (that depth was the one thing the judge
liked). Read the limb positions with a `preview_frame` (grid) at each
burst's ignite time, about 0.6 s after the block starts.

`text_behind_subject_tool` takes any overlay clip as `title_clip_id`, not
only titles: it puts a matted copy of the dancer above the overlay track, so
every burst on that track sits behind her. Fade the cut-out with the clip.

Fallback for a closer look at glass: `add_3d_scene_tool` `glass_hero`
`shape: "rings"` on `transparent` gives real refracting glass, but two
linked rings turning in place, not an expanding wave: use it as a product
accent, not for this.

## Timing & taste rules

- One ring per beat, 2-3 s apart (here 2.5 s); block 2.2 s, it ignites
  about 0.6 s in, so the sound and the limb position are read at
  block start + 0.6 s.
- The origin is a limb that is moving *outward* at that moment (a raised
  hand, a kicking foot); the ring does not follow it afterwards, so pick a
  moment where the limb holds or ends its move.
- `gravity: 0`, 24 particles: the rings are the point, the dust is texture.
- Cool, light colours (cyan, violet, white) on a warm-lit or single-hue
  dancer; rings in the dancer's own hue disappear (the judge called the
  second burst "barely visible").
- End on a ring decaying into a 1 s fade, not mid-move.

## Sound for it

Each ring is a cue: a reverse swell into a synth sub drop on the ignite
frame, a different `pitch` each time (-2, -5, -8) so the pulses do not
repeat. Keep the music bed 6 dB lower than for a hype edit (the judge did
not hear the subs under a busy drum loop): a sparse bass or pad bed suits
it better than a beat. `master_audio_tool` `iterate: true` added slaps,
punches and a door break on every block edge in the first pass; remove
those (`remove_clip_tool`) and master again.

## Common mistakes

- Rings in front of the body: reads as a flat overlay pasted on.
- Ring origin far from any limb (the point of the technique is that the
  body makes the sound).
- Identical hits on every ring.
- Expecting refraction: the block draws stroked circles; there is no lens
  distortion or dispersion of the footage under them.
