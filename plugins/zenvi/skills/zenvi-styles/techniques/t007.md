# t007 Rendered globe data take

## What it is

The data camera: a single 15-60 s shot orbiting a globe with streamlines, flight trails or a heat field,
no cuts, no titles beyond one clock. Colour and slow movement carry all the interest; narration (if any)
is sparse and the bed sits low.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. A supplied render or globe footage: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` (one long window); a slow push with
   `set_keyframes_tool` (scale 1.0 to 1.06 over the take) and `add_vignette_tool`.
2. Abstract trails over it: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "line_trails", "params": {"palette": "night"}}`
   (generative curves, not data).
3. The one clock: `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Header_3", "text": "MARCH 2024", "font": "Menlo"}` or the
   counter from t055.
4. VO with the bed 10-12 dB under: `duck_under_speech_tool`.

## Timing & taste rules

- One take, 15-60 s; camera moves at constant slow speed along a route (region to region).
- Only one text element; no lower thirds.

## Sound for it

A pad or ambient score that swells once at the reveal; no cut sounds (there are no cuts).

## Common mistakes

- Generative trails presented as real data: label them as illustration or use a real render.
