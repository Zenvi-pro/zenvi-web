# t009 Macro time-lapse as proof

## What it is

The visual proof in a science talk: after the claim ("plants move"), three macro time-lapses of 4-6 s each
(mushrooms rising, vines reaching, a flower opening) make the invisible visible. The first pushes in, the next
tilts. Held long enough to watch the change happen.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Footage: `gather_stock_footage_tool` `{"queries": ["flower blooming time lapse", "mushroom growing time lapse",
   "seedlings growing time lapse"], "min_seconds": 5}`.
2. Three shots of 4-6 s: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool`; speed up a real-time clip with `set_clip_speed_tool` (4-20x).
3. Camera: `set_keyframes_tool` `scale_x`/`scale_y` 1.0 to 1.08 over the shot (`ease_in_out`); a tilt with
   `location_y` 0.03 to -0.03.
4. Claim before, proof after: the VO line ends as the first time-lapse starts, then silence for the proof.

## Timing & taste rules

- 4-6 s each (median about 5 s); not under 2 s, the change has to be seen.
- Push-ins 5-8%, eased; one camera move type per shot.

## Sound for it

The bed swells gently under the proof (see the science style); no cut sounds.

## Common mistakes

- Time-lapses cut at 1 s: they become a montage, not proof.
