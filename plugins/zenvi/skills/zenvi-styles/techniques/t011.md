# t011 Transcript-keyed stock B-roll insert

## What it is

Every abstract claim gets a literal picture: "funds buy houses with cash" cuts to a house for 1.2 s and to
cash for 1.2 s, while the narration (or the A-roll speaker) runs continuously underneath. One stock query per
sentence, an insert about every 6 s in talking pieces, almost every clause in faceless news shorts.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Trigger words: `get_transcript_tool` `{"clipId": "<voice>", "includeWords": true}` (or phrase times of the VO).
2. One query per sentence: `gather_stock_footage_tool` `{"queries": ["house for sale sign", "counting money"],
   "orientation": "portrait"}`.
3. Inserts above the A-roll at the noun: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` `{"file_id": "<broll>", "track": "2",
   "position_seconds": <noun start>, "start_seconds": 1, "end_seconds": 2.2}`.
4. Mute every insert: `set_clip_audio_video_tool` `{"timeline_clip_ids": [...], "audio": "off"}`.
5. `review_edit_tool`: any insert that does not show its noun gets `swap_shot_tool`.

## Timing & taste rules

- Insert starts on the noun's first syllable (±2 frames), lasts 0.8-1.5 s.
- Literal beats generic: the insert must show the noun, not the topic.
- Talking pieces: at most one insert per 5-7 s so the face still carries trust.

## Sound for it

None of its own (muted); captions continue over the insert.

## Common mistakes

- Generic city B-roll on every line.
- Inserts with their own sound left on.
