# t012 2.5D paper-collage parallax

## What it is

Still archive made to move: photos cut into layers (background, mid card, foreground figure), each drifting
at its own rate so the camera seems to travel through them, unified by paper grain, a vignette and drop
shadows. A tram or a figure crossing the frame can act as the wipe into the next collage.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Background plate on track 1, a photo card on track 2, the foreground cut-out on track 3
   (`add_clip_to_timeline_tool`, `"track": "2"` / `"3"`). Real cut-outs: `generate_subject_matte_tool` on the
   figure first (people only); a framed card is enough for photos of objects.
2. Size and place the upper layers: `set_clip_property_tool` `scale_x`/`scale_y` 0.4-0.55 and `location_y`.
3. Drift each layer at its own rate, same direction, foreground fastest:
   `set_keyframes_tool` `{"timeline_clip_id": "<fg>", "property": "location_x", "ease": "ease_in_out",
   "points": [{"seconds": 0, "value": 0.34}, {"seconds": 6, "value": 0.18}]}` (mid card 0.1 of travel,
   background 0.04 plus a 1.10 to 1.14 scale push).
4. Depth cues: `apply_look_preset_tool` `{"timeline_clip_id": "<card>", "look": "shadow_soft"}` on the cut-outs;
   one grade (`color_grade_clip_tool` `look: "black_and_white"` or a warm sepia) across every layer.
5. Texture: `apply_look_preset_tool` `{"scope": "all", "look": "film_grain_16mm_classic"}`,
   `add_vignette_tool` `{"amount": 0.5}`; a paper rustle `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "paper"}` as it starts.

## Timing & taste rules

- Rates differ by at least 2x between neighbouring layers; total foreground travel 10-20% of the frame in 5-8 s.
- Always eased (ease_in_out); linear drift reads as a slideshow pan.
- 4-8 s per collage; cut or wipe to the next before the motion stops.

## Sound for it

A room tone or vinyl crackle bed, a paper rustle on each new collage, music low (bed 12+ dB under the VO).

## Common mistakes

- Every layer moving at the same rate: it is just a pan of a flat picture.
- Layers with different grades: the collage falls apart; grade them all the same.
