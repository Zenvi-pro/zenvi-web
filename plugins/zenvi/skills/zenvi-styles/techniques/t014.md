# t014 Scale-ladder zoom

## What it is

A descent (or ascent) through scales: Earth, a city, a street, a plant, its cells; or a pin, a pinhead, an
atom. Each level is a push-in that hands off to the next level through a zoom-through, at an equal time per
level so the viewer reads magnitude from time.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. One image or clip per scale level: `gather_stock_footage_tool` `{"queries": ["earth from space",
   "city lights aerial", "suburb aerial", "plant leaf macro", "cells microscope"]}` or
   `generate_image_and_add_to_timeline_tool` for levels stock cannot supply (keep the focal point centred).
2. Place them back to back, equal lengths (1.5-2 s for a montage, 6-10 s for a lecture pace):
   `add_clip_to_timeline_tool`.
3. Push into each level, accelerating: `set_keyframes_tool` `scale_x` and `scale_y`, `"ease": "ease_in"`,
   points 1.0 at 0 s to 1.35 at the clip end.
4. Hand off at every join: `apply_motion_transition_tool` `{"preset": "zoom_through", "track": "1",
   "time": <cut>}` (`focus_x`/`focus_y` on the next level's subject).
5. `add_sfx_tool` `whoosh_short` on each join (vary `seed`), a low pad or pulse under all of it.

## Timing & taste rules

- Equal time per level (the rule is the magic): 10x per 10 s in Powers of Ten, 1.6 s per level in a short.
- Each level's subject sits where the previous push ends (centre unless you set focus).
- 4-7 levels; end on the most extreme scale and hold it 1-2 s.

## Sound for it

A whoosh per join getting slightly brighter as you go deeper; one sustained pad; optional ui_tap when a
scale readout snaps (see t067).

## Common mistakes

- Linear pushes: the zoom stalls at each join; use ease_in into the transition.
- Mismatched focal points: the eye jumps at every join.
