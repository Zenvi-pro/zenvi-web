# t021 Dark silhouette to headlight reveal

## What it is

A 2-4 s hold of near-black with only a rim or ambient glow, then the lights
fire on one hit sound. The opening of most car hero films, and any product
with its own light (LED strip, screen, lamp). One per film, at the top.

## Build it in Zenvi

The source must contain the light-on moment: Zenvi cannot relight a lit
shot. Find it by luma: the frame where the clip's brightness jumps (a
`preview_frame` before and after). Trim so the tease runs 2-4 s before it,
and cut out *while the light is still on*: a light that goes off again
before the cut kills the payoff.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<headlight clip>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 1.2, "end_seconds": 5.8, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<hero drive shot, same car>", "position_seconds": 4.6, "start_seconds": 7.0, "end_seconds": 11.6, "track": "1"}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<tease>", "exposure": -0.3, "shadows": -0.5, "contrast": 0.25, "saturation": 0.6}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<hero>", "contrast": 0.15, "saturation": 0.8, "exposure": 0.1}
add_vignette_tool {"amount": 0.5, "feather": 0.5}
fade_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<tease>", "fade_in_seconds": 0.8}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<hero>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 4.6, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 9.2, "value": 1.06}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<hero>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 4.6, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 9.2, "value": 1.06}]}
```

The light-on hit is layered on the exact frame (here 3.2 s):

```json
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "riser_tonal", "at_seconds": 3.2, "duration": 2.2, "gain_db": -18}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "hit_transient", "at_seconds": 3.2, "gain_db": -8}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "impact_boom", "at_seconds": 3.2, "pitch": -5, "gain_db": -6}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "tail_bloom", "at_seconds": 3.2, "gain_db": -14}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

Fallback without a light-on take: a feathered `add_shape_mask_tool`
ellipse that grows over the lamp (`keyframes` on `grow`, 6-8 frames) on the
crushed shot, with the same hit; it reads as a light sweep, not a lamp
firing, so say so.

## Timing & taste rules

- Tease 2-4 s; first light within 5 s of the start (references: 5.1 s,
  15.9 s on a 60 s film).
- Crush only the tease (exposure -0.3, shadows -0.5). The shot after it
  plays at normal exposure: the jump from dark to lit is the point, and a
  hero shot graded as dark as the tease reads as muddy.
- Cut on a lit frame, never after the light dies. The next shot is the
  same car (a different model breaks the reveal: the judge flagged it).
- Vignette 0.4-0.5; no flash frame, no glow overlay; the lamp is the flash.

## Sound for it

One hit, layered: a short riser ending on the frame, a transient (relay
click) plus a low boom (`impact_boom` pitch -5) on it, a bloom tail. Music
is a low drone under the tease, entering properly after the light. The
dossier's films put sound on only 20-60 % of cuts: the light-on is one of
the cuts that always gets it.

## Common mistakes

- Light switching off again before the cut (the judge's first fix).
- A thin hit with no low end: `impact_thud` alone sounds hollow.
- Grading the whole film as dark as the tease.
- Text over the tease; the first type comes later or not at all.
