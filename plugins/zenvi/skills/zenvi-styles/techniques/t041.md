# t041 Digital punch-in jump cut

## What it is

One locked-off take becomes two "cameras": at a sentence boundary the frame jumps to a 115-140% crop for
the emphatic line, then jumps back out on the next idea. It hides jump cuts after `remove_silence_tool` and
gives a single-camera interview rhythm. Shines in interview shorts and talking-head explainers.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Tighten first: `remove_silence_tool` (or `remove_words_tool` for fillers) on the talking clip.
2. Find the sentence starts: `get_transcript_tool` `{"clipId": "<talk>", "includeWords": true}`. Without
   ASR on this surface (CLI portable), use the narration's line times (one TTS/VO file per line).
3. Cut at the pause before the key sentence and again before the next: `split_clip_tool`
   `{"clip_id": "<talk>", "position_seconds": <t1>}` and at `<t2>`; or place the three pieces with
   `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` (`start_seconds`/`end_seconds`) back to back.
4. Punch the middle piece: `set_clip_property_tool` `{"clip_id": "<mid>", "property": "scale_x", "value": 1.25}`
   and the same for `scale_y`. No ease, no keyframes: the jump is the effect. If the face drifts off centre at
   1.25, also set `location_y` (-0.05 lifts the face toward the top third).
5. Captions over all pieces: `kinetic_captions_tool` `{"cues": [...], "style": "documentary"}` (news) or
   `"creator"` (creator talking head).

## Timing & taste rules

- Crop 115-125% for a medium shot, up to 140% from a wide; never two punch levels in a row.
- Cut 1-3 frames before the first word of the line, never mid-word.
- One punch per 2-9 s of speech; hold the punch for the whole sentence, return on the next idea.
- Source must be at least 1080p for a 125% crop in a 1080 export (720p sources soften visibly).

## Sound for it

Nothing: the cut is invisible to the ear. Keep room tone continuous across both cuts (no fades on the audio).

## Common mistakes

- Punching on every sentence: the rhythm turns into a bounce. Alternate, and leave some lines wide.
- Keyframed slow zoom instead of a cut: reads as a drift, not a second camera.
- Cropping the head off on 9:16: check the frame at the punch with `watch_clip_tool` or `review_edit_tool`.
