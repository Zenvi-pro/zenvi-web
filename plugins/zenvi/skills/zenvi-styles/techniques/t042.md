# t042 Scale-alternating shot rotation

## What it is

The documentary assembly grammar: a wide sets the place (3-7 s), a medium follows the subject, a 1.5-3.5 s
macro or detail insert shows the work, then a wide or a reaction resets. Scale changes on almost every cut,
so even hard cuts feel motivated. Shines in observational documentary, recipes and tours.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Label the shots by scale: `search_clip_scenes_tool` `{"query": "wide shot of the village"}`,
   `"close-up of hands"` etc.; or `get_clips_with_full_metadata_tool` when the bin is indexed.
2. Place in the rotation W-M-D-W-M with `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` (`start_seconds`/`end_seconds`), lengths
   wide 3-5 s, medium 2-3 s, detail 1.5-2 s. `slice_clip_at_best_match_tool` picks the best window of a long take.
3. Natural sound per shot: keep each clip's own audio if it has sync sound; otherwise one ambience bed
   (`add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "room_tone", "at_seconds": 0, "align": "start", "duration": <len>, "gain_db": -24}`)
   plus specific foley spotted on visible actions.
4. One grade over all of it: `unify_grade_tool` `{"look": "elegant_dark_cinema", "look_intensity": 0.5}`.
5. `review_edit_tool`: fix any two same-scale shots in a row with `swap_shot_tool`.

## Timing & taste rules

- Never two shots of the same scale back to back unless it is a deliberate cut on action.
- Details 1.5-3 s; wides at least twice the detail length; a reset wide or reaction every 10-20 s.
- Documentary material sits around 14 cuts per minute; do not mix it with 40+/min graphic pacing in one piece.

## Sound for it

Diegetic only: each shot's own sound (hooves, tools, wind). No whooshes on cuts (`auto_sound_design_tool`
adds cut-synced whooshes this style does not use; if you run it, `intensity: "subtle"`, `genre: "documentary"`).

## Common mistakes

- All mediums: the eye never rests or learns the place.
- Details held 4+ s: the insert becomes a shot of its own and stalls the scene.
- Silent stock under a quiet scene: add ambience, or the cut rhythm feels empty.
