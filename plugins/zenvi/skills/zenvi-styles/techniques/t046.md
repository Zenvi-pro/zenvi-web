# t046 Tighten then release into held payoff

## What it is

A run of shots that get shorter each cut (2.2, 1.4, 0.9, 0.6, 0.4 s), then
one long hold (2.3-6.5 s) on the payoff. The squeeze builds pressure; the
hold lets the viewer breathe and look. Car films before the hero angle,
travel montages before the vista, weddings before the kiss.

## Build it in Zenvi

Pick the payoff first: the widest, cleanest shot of the *same* subject the
inserts showed. Lay the run from the top, each clip shorter than the last,
with no gaps.

## Timing & taste rules

- Each shot 55-70 % of the one before; the last two can repeat the
  shortest length (0.4 s, 12 frames). Never under 8 frames in a car film.
- Hold 2.3-6.5 s (4.5 s here), with a 3-5 % push so it does not freeze.
- The hold is the same subject as the inserts. A different car on the
  payoff scored 3.3; the same car's flank scored 5.9.
- Grade the run and the hold as one family; the release is space, not a
  new look. Keep the hold a touch brighter (+0.05 to +0.2 exposure).
- One squeeze per 30-60 s; repeated, it turns into a pattern.

## Sound for it

A steady bed under the whole run, a riser ending on the release frame, a
200-300 ms suck-out before it and one low boom on it. Nothing else on the
hold: the quiet after the hit is the release. Mechanical close-mic cues
(t078) can ride the longer inserts, not the 0.4 s ones.

## Common mistakes

- Payoff of a different subject or grade (the judge's first fix in pass 1).
- Stock clip audio left on: mute the picture clips.
- A hit on every cut of the squeeze; only the release gets the hit.
- A thin boom on the release: layer a sub or engine idle under it.
