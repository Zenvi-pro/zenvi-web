# t049 Wide-to-macro momentum cut

## What it is

A locked-off wide of a subject moving across frame cuts to a tracked macro
of the moving part (feet, wheels, hands) travelling the same direction, so
the motion carries over the cut. A stabilised tabletop moment (a pour)
interrupts for about 3.5 s with the music dropped out, then the movement
resumes. Brand stories about effort and craft.

`{"scale_x": -1}`) only when there is no text or logo in it.

## Timing & taste rules

- Wide 1.5-2.5 s, locked off; the subject enters or crosses the frame.
- Cut while the subject is mid-stride; the macro picks up the same phase.
- Same screen direction on both sides of the cut, always.
- The interruption is still and stabilised, about 3.5 s, music out by
  25-30 dB within 120 ms, back on the next motion cut.

## Sound for it

Bed under the movement; on the interruption only foley (the pour, the cup)
and room tone (t081). The music returns on the resume cut, with no riser.

## Common mistakes

- Direction flips across the cut.
- A handheld wide: the contrast with the tracked macro is lost.
- Fading the music slowly into the pour instead of dropping it.
