# t051 Borrowed-credibility source card

## What it is

Proof from a third party: a headline card (outlet name, accent rule, headline, date), a dark-mode social post
or a client email, centred over the B-roll for 3-10 s while the plate dims, sliding up with a whoosh. In a
vertical news short it lands at the claim beat (65-90% of runtime).

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Dim the plate: `add_vignette_tool` `{"amount": 0.6}` on the B-roll under the card.
2. The card, built from text: `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Gray_Box_2", "text": "<headline>",
   "subtitle": "<Outlet> · <date>", "screen_position": "center", "position_seconds": <t>,
   "duration_seconds": 4.5}`. A real card image (screenshot you are allowed to use): `import_stock_media_tool`
   `{"local_path": "<card.png>"}` then `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` on track 2.
3. Slide up and fade in: `set_keyframes_tool` on the card clip, `location_y` 0.25 to 0 over 0.35 s
   (`"ease": "ease_out"`) and `alpha` 0 to 1 over 0.2 s.
4. Sound: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "whoosh_short", "at_seconds": <t + 0.1>}`.
5. Pill badges on claims every 2-3 s: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "notification_stack", ...}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- Hold long enough to read the headline twice (about 0.3 s per word, min 3 s).
- Card at 70-85% of the frame width on 9:16, centred, with a shadow; the plate darker by 30-50%.
- Never invent a source: the headline, outlet and date must be real and supplied by the user.

## Sound for it

A short whoosh on the slide, the bed ducked a few dB while it is read.

## Common mistakes

- Fake outlet logos or made-up headlines (never).
- A card too small to read on a phone.
