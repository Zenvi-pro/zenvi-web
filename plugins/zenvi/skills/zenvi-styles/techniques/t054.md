# t054 Contextual unit morph

## What it is

Unit continuity: an icon chart (1 icon = 1,000 people) dissolves into archival footage while two or three
icons stay exactly where they were, then the footage dissolves back into the chart with the icons aligned. The
same unit travels between abstraction and reality, which is what made the best map/data film stand out.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The chart: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "chart_build", "placement": "full_frame", "track": "1",
   "params": {"kind": "bar", ...}}` (bars, not pictograms: see Missing).
2. Footage after it on the same track, then a 0.5-1 s dissolve on the join: `apply_motion_transition_tool`
   `{"preset": "blur_dissolve", "track": "1", "time": <join>, "duration": 0.8}`.
3. The held units on a track above, spanning the join: `add_emoji_tool` `{"emoji": "factory",
   "screen_position": "top_left", "size": 0.12, "position_seconds": <before join>, "duration_seconds": <past join>}`
   (or your own icon PNG on track 2 with `add_clip_to_timeline_tool`).
4. `add_sfx_tool` `whoosh_medium` on the first dissolve only.

## Timing & taste rules

- Dissolves 0.5-1 s; the held icons do not move at all across the join.
- The footage shows the thing the unit counts, within 1 s of the VO naming it.

## Sound for it

One whoosh on the first dissolve; later dissolves silent. VO 10-12 dB over the bed.

## Common mistakes

- Icons that drift or rescale across the join: continuity is the whole effect.
