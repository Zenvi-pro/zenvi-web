# t055 Data clock and HUD band

## What it is

The time variable is always visible exactly once, in one fixed spot: a ghost year at about 35% height behind
the data, a 6% date badge bottom-left, or a 3.5% month stamp top-right. It steps, never eases. A translucent
band over the bottom 8-10% carries the legend (colour ramp with units) and credit, turning a render into a
measurement. Scrubbing the clip backwards with the clock running back is the rhetorical rewind.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The clock: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "counter", "position_seconds": 0.3, "duration_seconds": 6.4,
   "params": {"values": ["1990","2000","2010","2020"], "style": "count", "label": "YEAR", "align": "left",
   "display_font": "mono", "palette": "mono"}}` (`style: "count"` changes digits in place).
2. A static stamp: `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Header_3", "text": "MARCH 2024", "font": "Menlo", ...}`.
3. The band and legend: `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Footer_2", "text": "Surface temperature anomaly -1 °C ... +1 °C"}`
   (a text legend; real colour ramps need the gap).
4. Rewind: `reverse_clip_tool` on a copy of the capture plus `speed_ramp_tool` (`ramp_in`) for the rush-slow feel.

## Timing & taste rules

- Exactly one clock on screen; it hard-steps on the data interval; mono or tabular numerals.
- Band 8-10% of height, 50-70% black; legend type 1.8-2.5% height.

## Sound for it

Nothing per step (a tick per year gets old fast); a quiet bed 10-12 dB under the VO.

## Common mistakes

- An odometer roll on years: data clocks step.
- Two time readouts (year in a title and in the band).
