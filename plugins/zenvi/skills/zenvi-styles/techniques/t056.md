# t056 Gag inside a static letter

## What it is

The alphabet card's joke: a big letter (45-70% of frame height) holds still in the centre while something
happens around or through it: a burst between background and letter, claw slashes tearing the paper, a lid
sliding off. Each card's gag has its own sting, 0.2-3 s into the card.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The letter card: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "B", "style": "stack", "animation": "fade",
   "emphasis": "none", "background": "#3b1f6b", "ink": "#c6ff3d", "text_size": 0.3,
   "position_seconds": <t>, "duration_seconds": 5}`.
2. The gag on top: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "particle_burst", "position_seconds": <t + 1.2>,
   "duration_seconds": 1.6, "params": {"shape": "confetti", "count": 140, "label": "BOO!"}}`; a second
   beat with `shape_morph` (`"shapes": ["burst", "blob"]`) or an image layer slid with `set_keyframes_tool`.
3. Sting: `add_sfx_tool` `impact_boom` on the burst, `cloth` or `paper` on the second beat.

## Timing & taste rules

- Every card the same length (see t090); the gag starts 0.2-3 s in (median about 1 s).
- The letter never moves during the gag; the gag sits between background and letter or around it.
- A different gag and a different sting per card.

## Sound for it

One dedicated sting per card (about 1.2 cues per cut), loud enough to be the joke's punchline, -1 dBTP max.

## Common mistakes

- Moving the letter with the gag: it stops being the anchor.
- The same burst on every card.
