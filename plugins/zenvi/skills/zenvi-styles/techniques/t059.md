# t059 HUD chips and scramble-decode text

## What it is

Over live footage (hands on a keyboard, a device in use), a run of small
tech overlays: code or hex tags with thin leader lines for 1.5-4 s, a
frosted notification pill ("Activated") for about 2 s, then a phrase that
scrambles for about 15 frames and resolves. It says "the product is
working" inside a human brand film.

## Build it in Zenvi

Three over-video blocks in sequence, each slightly overlapping the next,
on footage graded a little darker and cooler so thin white lines read.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<hands typing>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 3, "end_seconds": 10, "track": "1"}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<footage>", "exposure": -0.35, "contrast": 0.15, "saturation": 0.6, "temperature": -0.3}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "hud_callouts", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 2.6,
  "params": {"title": "SESSION 0x3F2A", "labels": ["KEY 7A:3F", "SYNC", "LAT"], "values": ["verified", "live", "12 ms"],
             "points": [{"x": 0.35, "y": 0.55}, {"x": 0.62, "y": 0.5}, {"x": 0.5, "y": 0.75}], "grid": "crosshair", "palette": "dark", "accent": "#3FE0FF"}}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "notification_stack", "position_seconds": 2.5, "duration_seconds": 2.5,
  "params": {"items": ["Workspace: Activated"], "app": "Relay", "time": "now", "position": "right", "palette": "light", "accent": "#3FE0FF"}}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "text_scramble", "position_seconds": 4.7, "duration_seconds": 2.5,
  "params": {"text": "NOW FULL-TIME", "glyphs": "symbols", "size": 110, "palette": "dark", "accent": "#3FE0FF", "exit": false}}
```

Change copy or timing afterwards with `update_motion_block_tool`
(`params` merge over the old ones). The overlays are not tracked: pick a
locked-off shot, or the chips float off the hands (needs
`tracked-point-callout-pin`).

## Timing & taste rules

- Chips 1.5-4 s, pill about 2 s, scramble about 15 frames of noise then at
  least 1 s of the resolved phrase. The test's 1.5 s scramble block cut
  off before the phrase could be read: use 2.5 s.
- Points on the action (hands, screen), never on empty space.
- `hud_callouts` lines and labels are thin and small: on a bright plate
  they vanish. Darken the plate (exposure -0.3 or more) or use a dark
  shot.
- One accent (cyan here) across all three blocks.

## Sound for it

A quiet `ui_tap` (-15 dB) on each chip anchor, `notification_blip` on the
pill, `glitch_burst` (0.5 s, -14 dB) under the scramble and a
`hit_transient` when the phrase resolves. Keep the music low and
instrumental; a vocal bed fights the UI sounds.

## Common mistakes

- All three overlays at once: one at a time, overlapping by 0.1-0.3 s.
- A scramble that never holds its resolved text.
- Chips pinned to a moving object without tracking.
