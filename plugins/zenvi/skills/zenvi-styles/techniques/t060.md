# t060 Headline hook card at start

## What it is

Context for muted viewers: from frame 1 (at most 0.5 s in) a 3-6 word hook or headline sits in a dark
rounded card or as bold type in the upper third for 3.7-5 s, then cuts out exactly as the speaker's name
lower third cuts in. It names the thesis in plain words before anyone has to listen.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Hook: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "Who really owns your street?", "style": "creator", "animation": "pop",
   "emphasis": ["owns"], "y": 0.25, "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 3.7}`; a boxed card instead:
   `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Gray_Box_2", ...}` or `add_kinetic_title_tool` with `background`.
2. Hand-off: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "lower_third", "position_seconds": 3.7, "duration_seconds": 3.3,
   "params": {"name": "Dana Ruiz", "role": "Housing economist"}}` at the same instant the hook leaves.
3. Captions below the lower third from then on: `kinetic_captions_tool` `{"style": "documentary"}`.
4. Sound: `add_sfx_tool` `pop` on the hook (0.05 s) and `whoosh_short` on the hand-off.

## Timing & taste rules

- Hook visible by 0.5 s, readable in under 2 s (3-6 words), off by 5 s.
- Upper third on 9:16 (clear of the bottom UI band), never over the face.
- The hook states the thesis, not the topic ("Who really owns your street?" not "Housing market update").

## Sound for it

A small pop on the first frame, a whoosh or nothing on the hand-off; the voice starts within 0.5 s.

## Common mistakes

- A logo or channel card for the first 3-8 s instead of the claim.
- Hook text that stays for the whole clip (that is a permanent bug, see the finish checklist).
