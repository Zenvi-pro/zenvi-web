# t061 Logo end card and lockup

## What it is

The film's single ending: on the last action (or after a short fade to
black) the mark opens, the wordmark slides out, one hit or sub drop lands
as it locks, and the card holds 1.4-7 s to the last frame. Every ad,
brand film and branded clip ends this way.

## Build it in Zenvi

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<last action shot>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 1, "end_seconds": 3.9, "track": "1"}
fade_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<last shot>", "fade_out_seconds": 0.5}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "logo_reveal_end", "position_seconds": 2.9, "duration_seconds": 3.6, "track": "2", "placement": "full_frame",
  "params": {"brand": "<brand>", "logo": "<transparent PNG file id>", "cta": "", "url": "<url>", "glow": true, "palette": "dark", "accent": "<brand colour>"}}
fade_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<logo block>", "fade_in_seconds": 0.3}
fade_clip_tool {"clip_id": "<music>", "fade_out_seconds": 0.15}
```

End the music clip on the hit (trim it to end 0.1-0.2 s after the lock
frame): the hard stop plus the boom is the button. Variants:
`logo_build` (dots morph into a mark), `ripple_logo` (a sting between
scenes), or `text_behind_subject_tool` with the brand word behind the last
person on screen.

## Timing & taste rules

- Lockup 3-7 s, at most about 10 % of a film over 50 s; it holds to the
  last frame, no fade-out, nothing after it.
- One ending: the mark, the wordmark, one URL or CTA, not three.
- The URL is small in `logo_reveal_end`: keep it to a short domain, or
  set it as its own `add_title_tool` at 36 px or more at 1080 (the judge
  could not read it on a phone).
- Dark lockup after a night film, light after a light film: the end card
  shares the film's grade.

## Sound for it

On the lock frame: `impact_boom` (pitch -4, -7 dB), `sub_drop` (-10 dB),
`tail_bloom` (-13 dB), with a `reverse_swell` (-16 dB) ending on it. The
music stops on the hit; the tail carries the hold. A cut vocal needs a
reverb tail or a cut on a phrase end.

## Common mistakes

- Music running under the hold, or fading out later than the hit.
- Fading the logo out at the end.
- A logo that appears before the brief's last story beat is over.
- Tiny, low-contrast URL text.
