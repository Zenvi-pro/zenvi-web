# t062 One feature, one metaphor, one line

## What it is

Each feature gets one 3-5 s unit: a picture of the claim (lines orbiting
for spatial sound, rings for silence, a filling bar for battery), one
sentence-case line of about 4 % frame height, then a hard cut on an onset
into the next feature. The middle of a CGI product ad.

## Build it in Zenvi

Cut on the music: `detect_beats_tool`, then units of two bars (3.85 s at
124.8 BPM). The metaphor is a motion block; the line is a quiet title.

```json
detect_beats_tool {"file_id": "<music>"}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "line_trails", "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 3.85, "track": "1",
  "params": {"pattern": "orbit", "count": 10, "turns": 4, "size": 0.8, "palette": "dark", "accent": "#8FB8FF", "exit": false}}
add_kinetic_title_tool {"text": "Sound that turns with you.", "position_seconds": 0.6, "duration_seconds": 3.25, "style": "documentary", "animation": "rise", "text_size": 0.042, "y": 0.86}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "gradient_field", "position_seconds": 3.85, "duration_seconds": 3.85, "track": "1",
  "params": {"style": "orbs", "palette": "dark", "speed": 0.6, "colors": ["#1B2A4A", "#2E3F6B"], "exit": false}}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "particle_burst", "position_seconds": 4.2, "duration_seconds": 2.4,
  "params": {"shape": "rings", "count": 40, "rings": true, "gravity": 0, "colors": ["#8FB8FF", "#FFFFFF"]}}
add_kinetic_title_tool {"text": "The room goes quiet when you press play.", "position_seconds": 4.45, "duration_seconds": 3.25, "style": "documentary", "animation": "rise", "text_size": 0.042, "y": 0.86}
```

With a product render or 3D scene (`add_3d_scene_tool`), put the product
in the unit and the line beside it; the metaphor graphic is the weak part
without real CGI.

## Timing & taste rules

- Unit 3-5 s; the line enters 0.4-0.6 s after the cut and stays to it.
- One line, sentence case, 6-9 words, about 4 % of frame height
  (`text_size` 0.04), never two sizes in a unit.
- Hard cuts only, on a downbeat; same palette in every unit.
- Three to five units, then the product or the end card.

## Sound for it

A soft `swish_airy` or `impact_thud` (-12 dB) on each cut, one small
accent sound on the metaphor (`sparkle` -18 dB on the rings). Calm music
with a clear downbeat; no whooshes between units.

## Common mistakes

- Two lines of copy plus a headline in one unit.
- A graphic that does not picture the claim (random orbs).
- Transitions between units; this is a hard-cut rhythm.

## Verified

Not rendered (budget). The build above was scripted (line_trails orbit
unit, then a gradient_field plus particle_burst rings unit, two
documentary titles, 7.7 s at 1920x1080) but stopped before render when the
budget ran out; no judge score. The block parameters were checked against
`list_motion_blocks_tool`. Expect the metaphor graphics to read as
abstract rather than as the claim.
