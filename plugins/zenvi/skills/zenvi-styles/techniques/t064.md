# t064 Pop-in on spoken keyword

## What it is

"A plant needs three things: water, light and time": each noun gets its icon at the instant it is said,
scaling 0 to 110 to 100% over about 6 frames, held 0.4-1.3 s (or until the list completes), each with a click.
The picture answers the voice word for word.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Word times: `get_transcript_tool` `{"clipId": "<vo>", "includeWords": true}`; with generated VO, one
   file per phrase gives exact phrase times.
2. Each icon at its word: `add_emoji_tool` `{"emoji": "droplet", "screen_position": "center_left",
   "size": 0.18, "position_seconds": <word start>, "duration_seconds": <until the list ends>}`
   (`search_emojis_tool` finds names; your own icon PNG works with `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` on track 2+).
3. Pop with overshoot: `set_keyframes_tool` `{"timeline_clip_id": "<icon>", "property": "scale_x",
   "ease": "ease_out_back", "points": [{"seconds": 0, "value": 0}, {"seconds": 0.2, "value": 1.0}]}` and `scale_y`.
4. Click per icon: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "pop", "at_seconds": <word start>, "seed": <i>}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- Icon lands within ±2 frames of the word onset; 0.2 s pop; layout in a row or arc, left to right in speech order.
- One icon per item; same style for all icons (all emoji, or all line icons).

## Sound for it

A click or pop per icon, varied seeds, -14 to -18 dB under the voice.

## Common mistakes

- Icons arriving on a beat grid instead of the word.
- Mixed icon styles in one row.
