# t065 Progressive-reveal definition slide

## What it is

The lecture slide done right: a white card with a bold header (and two labels) is cut in; a few seconds later
the same card is cut in again with the definitions added under the labels. No fades, no motion: the
information build is the animation, and the viewer's eye goes straight to the new lines.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Header slide: `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Text_Card", "text": "Old mindset vs new data",
   "background_color": "#ffffff", "text_color": "#111111", "position_seconds": <t>, "duration_seconds": 2.5}`.
2. Same slide with lines added, hard cut: `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Standard_3", "lines": ["Old mindset
   vs new data", "Old: 'they' are poor", "New: most are in the middle"], "background_color": "#ffffff",
   "text_color": "#111111", "position_seconds": <t + 2.5>, "duration_seconds": 4}`. For an existing slide,
   duplicate it and `edit_title_tool` the copy so the header stays pixel-identical.
3. A click on the re-cut: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "mouse_click", "at_seconds": <t + 2.5>}`.
4. Better typography than the source: `font` and `font_weight: "bold"` on the header, labels 3-8% height.

## Timing & taste rules

- The header must not move between the two cuts (same template and position).
- Each added line is spoken as it appears; hold the full slide at least 3 s.
- Labels at least 3% of height; condensed caps or a clean sans, never default centred body text.

## Sound for it

A soft click or nothing; the voice carries it.

## Common mistakes

- Default centred sans at one size: the watched source is also the weakest-typography clip; aim higher.
- Animating each line in: it slows a technique whose strength is the instant build.
