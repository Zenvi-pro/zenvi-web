# t067 Scale HUD with decade-snap readout

## What it is

The Powers of Ten frame: a persistent reticle around the view, and readouts in the margin (metres and 10^n)
that snap, never animate, at every decade. One number changes per step at one fixed spot, so the viewer
reads scale from the readout and the steady interval.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The zoom: one clip per decade back to back, each with a linear pull-out or push-in
   (`set_keyframes_tool` `scale_x`/`scale_y`, `"ease": "linear"`, 1.3 to 1.0 over the interval); see t014.
2. The frame: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "hud_callouts", "duration_seconds": <whole>,
   "params": {"title": "", "labels": [], "values": [], "grid": "crosshair"}}`.
3. The readout, one title per decade at a fixed corner: `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Header_1",
   "text": "10³ m", "font": "Menlo", "text_color": "#ffffff", "position_seconds": <i * interval>,
   "duration_seconds": <interval>}`.
4. A tiny tick when it snaps: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "ui_tap", "at_seconds": <step>, "gain_db": -16}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- Equal intervals (2.5 s in a short, 10 s in the original); the readout changes exactly on the cut.
- Readout about 4% height, mono, same corner always; one readout only.

## Sound for it

Narration dry over silence or a thin pad; ticks optional and quiet.

## Common mistakes

- Animating the number (counter roll): the snap is the rhythm.
