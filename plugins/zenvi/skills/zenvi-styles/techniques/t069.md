# t069 Spring wordmark or CTA lockup and hold

## What it is

The landing: the hero or the mark drops in on an ease-in, overshoots to 105-115% with a spring bounce, a
hit and a short stinger land on the settle, and then the lockup holds completely still for 1.5-4.4 s. The
hold is what lets the brand register; an abrupt fade-out is the common failure.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The lockup: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "logo_reveal_end", "placement": "full_frame",
   "position_seconds": <t>, "duration_seconds": 4.5, "params": {"brand": "Little Lab",
   "cta": "Learn a word a day", "url": "", "palette": "sky", "glow": true}}` (holds to its last frame).
   With a real logo file: `params.logo` = its bin file id. Kids alternative: `logo_build`.
2. A word CTA: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "Subscribe", "animation": "pop", ...}` held 2 s.
3. Spring by hand on any clip: `set_keyframes_tool` `scale_x`/`scale_y` with `"ease": "ease_out_back"`
   (0.8 to 1.0 over 0.3 s) or `location_y` from above with `ease_in` then `ease_out_back`.
4. Sound: `add_sfx_tool` `impact_punch` (gain -12) on the settle and `sparkle` or a stinger 0.5 s later;
   `duck_tool` `{"under": "heroes"}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- Overshoot 105-115%, settle within 0.3 s; then nothing moves.
- Hold at least 1.5 s (kids 4 s); audio button within 0.3 s of the last visual change.
- One keyword for the ask.

## Sound for it

Hit on the landing, stinger tail, music resolving or ending on the lockup.

## Common mistakes

- Fading out the lockup straight away.
- Music running past the hold into silence-less cut-off: end the music on the card.
