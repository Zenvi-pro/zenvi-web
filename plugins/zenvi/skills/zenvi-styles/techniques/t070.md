# t070 Staggered map markers

## What it is

Locate the story: isolate a region on a map or globe, then pop 5-8 markers from 0 to 100% (ease-out) 0.2 s
apart with short labels, while the camera pushes slowly into the region.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. A map plate: a map image or footage (`add_clip_to_timeline_tool`; `gather_stock_footage_tool`
   `"world map"` or `generate_image_and_add_to_timeline_tool` for a styled map; you must place markers at
   real positions yourself).
2. The push: `set_keyframes_tool` `scale_x`/`scale_y` 1.0 to 1.25 over the beat, `ease_in_out`.
3. Markers with labels: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "hud_callouts", "params": {"title": "ICE CORE SITES",
   "labels": ["Vostok", "Dome C", "EPICA", "Law Dome", "Byrd"], "values": ["3,623 m", ...], "grid": "none",
   "points": [{"x": 0.35, "y": 0.4}, ...]}}` (anchor rings pop in sequence, labels decode in).
4. A tick per marker: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "ui_tap", "at_seconds": <t + i * 0.2>, "seed": <i>}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- 0.2 s between markers, ease-out pops, 5-8 markers; labels 3-4% height, caps or mono.
- Push 15-25% over 4-6 s; markers ride with the map (see Missing).

## Sound for it

Soft taps per marker under the VO; no whoosh.

## Common mistakes

- Markers on a decorative map at invented positions: only plot real places, at their real positions.
