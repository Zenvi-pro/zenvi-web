# t071 Tracked leader-line callout

## What it is

A thin leader line draws from a product part (a lens, a seam, a port)
out to empty space and a small two-line spec label reveals at its end,
held 1.4-2.5 s and removed before the cut. Tech reviews, car films and
explainers; one to three per film.

## Build it in Zenvi

`hud_callouts` over the video with `points` on the parts. It is static:
use a locked-off or very slow shot and find the part's position on a
frame of it (0-1 of the frame).

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<steady product close-up>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 7.2, "end_seconds": 10.0, "track": "1"}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "hud_callouts", "position_seconds": 0.2, "duration_seconds": 2.5,
  "params": {"title": "", "labels": ["1/1.3-INCH CMOS", "3-AXIS GIMBAL"], "values": ["F/1.7 APERTURE", "±0.01° SHAKE"],
             "points": [{"x": 0.49, "y": 0.6}, {"x": 0.62, "y": 0.42}], "grid": "none", "palette": "dark", "accent": "#FFFFFF", "exit": true}}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "mouse_click", "at_seconds": 0.55, "gain_db": -11}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "mouse_click", "at_seconds": 0.8, "gain_db": -13, "seed": 2}
```

The block runs 2.5 s at least (2.3 s is refused). On a moving shot the
anchor slides off the part: that needs `tracked-point-callout-pin`
(drive the block's points from a tracker); until then, steady shots only.

## Timing & taste rules

- 1.4-2.5 s readable, gone before the cut (`exit: true`, end the block
  0.1-0.2 s before the shot ends).
- Two callouts at most per shot; labels 2-3 % of frame height, caps,
  tracked mono.
- Labels over a light, busy area vanish (the judge could not read one
  over pale pavement): put labels where the plate is dark, or darken the
  plate a little with `color_grade_clip_tool`.
- `grid: "none"` for a product; the crosshair grid reads as a game HUD.

## Sound for it

A small `mouse_click` (-11 to -13 dB) as each anchor ring lands, a quiet
whoosh on the cut after. Nothing on the label text. Fade the music bed
0.3 s at the end of the test or cut on a phrase end.

## Common mistakes

- Callouts on a handheld or orbiting shot with no tracking.
- Long sentences in the label: two short lines only.
- A box behind every label; hairlines read as premium.
