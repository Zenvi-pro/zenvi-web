# t074 Drop-locked montage with hard stop

## What it is

A music-led science montage: a 1 s-per-clip build with glitch texture, then on the drop the frame becomes
2.35:1 letterbox, shots lengthen to about 1.2 s and a sub drop hits; at the end the music cuts dead to black
or to a title, no fade.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Beats and the drop: `detect_beats_tool` on the track (the drop = the biggest loudness jump).
2. The build: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` 0.9-1 s windows (or `build_reel_tool` style `hype`), then
   `apply_fx_tool` `{"clip_id": "<id>", "effects": [{"type": "glitch"}]}` on the build shots.
3. The drop: letterbox the later shots with `add_shape_mask_tool` `{"clip_ids": [...], "shape": "rectangle",
   "width": 1.0, "height": 0.75, "feather": 0}`; `add_sfx_tool` `sub_drop` and `impact_boom` (-10 dB) on the drop.
4. Hard stop: end the last shot and the music on the same frame (`automate_volume_tool` `fade_out` 60 ms).

## Timing & taste rules

- Build shots 0.8-1 s on the beat; post-drop shots 1.2-1.5 s.
- Letterbox, colour or speed change exactly on the drop frame (within 2 frames).
- End on a hard stop, but give the last image 1 s so it is not a cut-off (the references lost points here).

## Sound for it

Sub drop on the drop, a reverse swell into it, silence after the stop.

## Common mistakes

- Reveal and drop a few frames apart.
- Glitch on every shot after the drop too: the drop needs a change of texture.
