# t081 Foley spotted to in-frame actions

## What it is

Real recorded sounds on the actions the viewer sees (a pour, a cap, a
footstep, a fabric pull), about 5-12 per minute, sitting at or under the
music; whooshes only on real motion. Or foley alone, no music. It makes a
brand film feel physical rather than scored.

## Build it in Zenvi

Find each action's frame by eye (`watch_clip_window_tool`, a contact
sheet): no tool detects in-frame actions yet (`action-foley-spotter`).
Then pick real recordings: the kit for clicks, cloth, paper, thuds;
Freesound for pours and steps.

```json
search_sound_library_tool {"category": "cloth", "count": 4}
search_sound_library_tool {"category": "thud", "query": "wood", "count": 4}
search_freesound_music_tool {"query": "coffee pour cup"}
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<pour id>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 3.7, "start_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 3.3, "track": "6"}
search_freesound_music_tool {"query": "footsteps running asphalt"}
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<steps id>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 2.0, "start_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 1.6, "track": "4"}
add_sfx_tool {"provider": "kit", "sound_id": "<cup down id>", "at_seconds": 6.9, "gain_db": -14}
analyze_mix_tool {}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

Kit ids are the bare numbers from the search (no `kit:` prefix).

## Timing & taste rules

- Each cue within 2 frames of the visible contact.
- 5-12 cues per minute; skip actions that are tiny or off-screen.
- Foley 0-6 dB under the bed; when the music drops out, foley carries
  the moment at normal level.
- Whooshes only where something actually moves fast through frame.
- One recording per action type per scene; vary it for repeats.

## Sound for it

The foley is the sound design; the bed leaves room (`eq_tool`
`voice_pocket`-style dip or a lower level). Room tone under foley-only
passages so silence is not dead.

## Common mistakes

- Synth recipes where a real recording is needed (a pour from noise).
- Cues early or late by more than 2 frames.
- Foley above the bed everywhere (that is t078, for inserts only).
- Whooshes on static shots.
