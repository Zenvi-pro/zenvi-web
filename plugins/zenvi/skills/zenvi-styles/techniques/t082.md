# t082 L-cut audio carrying over a cutaway

## What it is

The sound bridge: show the speaker briefly, then cut the picture to what they describe while their voice
keeps running (audio leads or trails picture by 0.3-4 s). The footage becomes their illustration, and music
can enter under the cutaway. The main "transition" of interview-led essays.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Split the interview's sound from its picture: `separate_clip_audio_tool` `{"timeline_clip_ids": ["<int>"]}`.
2. Shorten the picture only: `trim_clips_tool` on the video part so it ends at the last word you want seen.
3. Cutaways on the picture track after it: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool`, then mute them:
   `set_clip_audio_video_tool` `{"timeline_clip_ids": [...], "audio": "off"}`.
4. Music in under the cutaway: place the bed at the picture cut, `set_track_role_tool` `music`,
   `automate_volume_tool` `{"role": "music", "shape": "fade_in", "length_ms": 1200}`; `duck_tool` under the voice.
5. Pre-lap (J-cut): `move_clips_tool` the next speaker's audio 0.3-1 s earlier than their picture.

## Timing & taste rules

- Hold the speaker 1.5-3 s first so the voice has a face; then 2-6 s per cutaway.
- Cut picture at a pause or breath, never mid-word; cutaway audio muted.
- Music bed 15-20 dB under the voice in long-form, 8-10 in shorts.

## Sound for it

The voice is the continuity; ambience of the cutaway may sit very low (-30 dB) for texture.

## Common mistakes

- Cutaways that keep their own sound: two voices of the world fight.
- Cutting picture and audio at the same frame every time (no bridges at all).
