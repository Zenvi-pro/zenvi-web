# t086 Sound cue per graphic or UI event

## What it is

The single biggest difference between a polished explainer and an average one: every graphic event has a
sound. Clicks on icons, pops on words, hits on landings, whooshes on moves, a riser into the reveal and a
stinger on the logo; 11-36 cues per minute, about 80% of them under the voice.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Optional palette: `create_sound_kit_tool` so every cue comes from one family.
2. Let the engine spot the events: `auto_sound_design_tool` `{"genre": "<saas_ui, video_essay or documentary>", "intensity": "normal"}` (titles, motion blocks and cuts get cues from the cue sheet).
3. Read what it did: `spot_sound_tool` `{}`; add what it missed with `add_sfx_tool`
   (`pop`, `mouse_click`, `ui_tap`, `whoosh_short`, `impact_punch`, `sparkle`, `riser_tonal`) at the event time,
   varying `seed` between repeats.
4. Mix: `set_track_role_tool` `sfx` for the cue track, `duck_tool` the bed under the voice,
   `analyze_mix_tool`, then `master_audio_tool` `{"preset": "social"}` last.

## Timing & taste rules

- Cue on the event frame (pops, landings) or with its peak on the move's midpoint (whooshes, `align: "peak"`).
- 11-36 per minute for explainers; documentary stays near 3-6 (diegetic only).
- Clicks and pops -14 to -20 dB under the voice; one hit per section at full level.

## Common mistakes

- The same sample on every event: vary seed and recipe.
- A whoosh on every hard cut in a calm piece (documentary): sound follows graphics, not cuts.
