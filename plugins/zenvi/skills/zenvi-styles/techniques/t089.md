# t089 Closing action cut to black packshot

## What it is

The last feature gives way to a physical action (a laptop lid shuts, buds
drop into a case, a cap clicks on), a foley snap on the frame of contact, and
a hard cut to a logo or packshot card on black held 2.6-11 s. Everything
stops on that frame: motion, music, grade. The standard ending of product
CGI and launch films.

## Build it in Zenvi

Find the contact frame first: step through the action at 5-10 fps around
where it lands (a `preview_frame` pair, or `slice_clip_at_best_match_tool`
with "lid fully closed"). The clip ends on that frame, not one after: a lid
that bounces open again, or a shot that runs on after it, loses the snap.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<lid closing shot>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 3.7, "track": "1"}
color_grade_clip_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<close>", "contrast": 0.15, "saturation": 0.85, "shadows": -0.15}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<close>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.7, "value": 1.08}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<close>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.7, "value": 1.08}]}
add_title_tool {"text": "<product or line>", "position_seconds": 2.7, "duration_seconds": 3.6, "background_color": "#000000", "text_color": "#F2F2F2", "font": "Helvetica Neue", "font_weight": "bold", "font_scale": 0.9, "template": "Standard_1"}
```

The music stops on the cut (import it to end exactly there), and the
contact is layered:

```json
import_stock_media_tool {"source": "freesound", "sound_id": "<ambient bed>", "preview_url": "<url>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 4, "duration_seconds": 2.7, "track": "4"}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "hit_transient", "at_seconds": 2.7, "gain_db": -6}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "sub_drop", "at_seconds": 2.7, "gain_db": -10}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "tail_bloom", "at_seconds": 2.7, "gain_db": -16}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

With a logo file, the card is a still of it on black (or `logo_reveal_end`
if it should animate; then it is no longer a hard held cut). Without a
closing action in the footage: end the last shot on any contact (a hand set
down, a door shut) with the same sound; a fade to black is not this
technique.

## Timing & taste rules

- Cut on the contact frame exactly; the closing move itself 1.5-3 s with a
  4-8 % push-in so the shot is still moving when it stops.
- Card 2.6-4 s for a social cut (references hold 2.6-11 s; the judge
  called 3.6 s of a dead static card "dead air", so give a longer card a
  slow 2-3 % scale or a late second line).
- Card: pure black, one line or logo, about 4-5 % of frame height, no
  animation in.
- Music ends on the cut; only the snap's tail rings into the black.

## Sound for it

Three layers on the contact frame: a loud short transient (the latch),
a sub drop for weight, a bloom tail of 1-2 s. Then silence. A swell or a
riser into it is wrong: the film should stop, not climax.

## Common mistakes

- Cutting a frame late, after the lid has settled or bounced.
- Music running on under the card.
- A fade instead of a hard cut, or a flash frame on the cut.
- A busy end card (tagline, URL and legal at full size).
