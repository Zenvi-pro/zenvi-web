# t091 Foley-first cold open

## What it is

The film starts with the work itself: an axe on ice, a gate, an engine, with only its own sound. No music,
no ambience bed, no title for the first 7-13 s; close, tactile pictures; speech enters on top later. It
promises authenticity before any claim is made.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Close shots of the action with their sync sound: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` (keep audio);
   `set_audio_gain_tool` so the production sound sits about -18 to -12 dBFS.
2. No sync sound: spot foley to the visible actions with `add_sfx_tool` (`hit_transient`, `cloth`, `paper`;
   or real recordings from `search_sound_library_tool`) at the exact frames, plus a quiet `room_tone`
   bed (-22 to -26 dB).
3. Voice or subtitle enters after 5-13 s; the music only at the first cutaway or the title (t123).

## Timing & taste rules

- 7-13 s before any music or title; first shot is close and tactile.
- Each foley within one frame of its action; nothing generic (no whooshes).

## Sound for it

This is all sound: diegetic only, dynamic (leave LRA high, about -18 to -20 LUFS for long-form).

## Common mistakes

- Starting music "to set the mood": it erases the authenticity.
- Foley out of sync with the action: worse than silence.
