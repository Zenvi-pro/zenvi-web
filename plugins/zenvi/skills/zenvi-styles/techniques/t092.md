# t092 Held-label calm pacing

## What it is

Let the image work: beautiful macro or nature footage cut on hard cuts every 4.2-4.8 s, one 0.8 s fade at the
very start, and an italic serif label at about 4% height that fades in over clean space and out before the cut.
No effects. The watched example lacked foley; adding texture sounds is the upgrade.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Shots of 4.5 s: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` (p10 1.4 s, p90 4.8 s: an occasional short shot is fine).
2. Open soft: `fade_clip_tool` `{"clip_id": "<first>", "fade_in_seconds": 0.8}`.
3. Label per shot in the clean band: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "Hibiscus, opening", "style": "editorial",
   "animation": "fade", "emphasis": "none", "text_size": 0.04, "y": 0.88,
   "position_seconds": <shot + 0.8>, "duration_seconds": 3.0}` (out before the cut).
4. Texture sound: `search_sound_library_tool` / `add_sfx_tool` for subtle organic textures; a calm bed.

## Timing & taste rules

- 4-5 s per shot, hard cuts, no transitions after the opening fade.
- Labels 3.5-4.5% height, italic serif, fade 0.4-0.6 s, gone 0.5 s before the cut.

## Sound for it

A calm bed 12-18 dB under any VO, gentle texture foley; no whooshes or hits.

## Common mistakes

- Speeding it up with transitions: the calm is the point.
