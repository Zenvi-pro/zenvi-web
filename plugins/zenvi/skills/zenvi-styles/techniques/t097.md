# t097 Dissolve on calm, hard cut on action

## What it is

A rule for choosing the join: every cut on a physical action (a jump, a splash, a hand landing) is a hard
cut; a dissolve is spent only where place, time or mood changes (the song ends and the episode begins, day to
night). The rarity of the dissolve is what makes it read as a chapter change.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Lay the action shots back to back (hard cuts): `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` with exact windows.
2. At the one place shift, a cross dissolve on the shared track: `add_transition_tool`
   `{"track": "1", "position_seconds": <cut - 0.2>, "duration_seconds": 0.4, "transition": "common/fade"}`
   (12 frames at 30 fps; up to 1 s for memory or wedding pieces). Change the length later with
   `update_transition_tool`. Alternative on any cut: `apply_motion_transition_tool` `{"preset": "blur_dissolve", ...}`.
3. Hand the sound over with the picture: fade the music out across the dissolve
   (`fade_clip_tool` `fade_out_seconds` on the music clip) and start the new ambience on the dissolve
   (`add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "room_tone", "align": "start", ...}` or the new scene's own sound).
4. A small transient on action cuts: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "hit_transient", "gain_db": -16}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- Dissolves 12 frames (0.4 s) to 1 s; at most one per 30-60 s in a cut-led piece.
- Never dissolve on an action: it softens the hit and reads as an amateur default.
- Crossfade the audio over the full dissolve; an abrupt sound switch under a soft picture change is the
  failure the reference was criticised for.

## Sound for it

Action cuts: a short transient or the action's own foley. The dissolve: music out, ambience in, no whoosh.

## Common mistakes

- Dissolves on every join ("default transition" look).
- Dissolve between two shots of the same place: there is no shift to mark.
