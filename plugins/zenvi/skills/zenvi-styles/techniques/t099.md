# t099 Graphic wipe at section change

## What it is

A designed join that says "new chapter": square tiles toggling across the frame, concentric rings, or a hard
vertical bar, over 8-18 frames, always on a section boundary and always with a sound. Used sparingly, it is
the explainer's chapter break; it alternates with glitch and white-flash joins.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Find a graphic luma wipe: `search_transitions_tool` `{"query": "squares"}` (or `"checker"`, `"circle"`,
   `"bars"`); take the result's id.
2. Put it on the cut: `add_transition_tool` `{"track": "1", "position_seconds": <cut - 0.25>,
   "duration_seconds": 0.5, "transition": "<id>"}`.
3. Or a moving join: `apply_motion_transition_tool` `{"preset": "glitch" | "flash_white" | "zoom_through",
   "track": "1", "time": <cut>}`.
4. Name the new section right after it: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "Part 2: the planet", "style":
   "stack", "position_seconds": <cut + 0.2>, "duration_seconds": 2.4}`.
5. Sound: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "whoosh_medium", "at_seconds": <wipe middle>}` then
   `{"recipe": "impact_punch", "at_seconds": <wipe end>, "gain_db": -12}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- 8-18 frames (0.27-0.6 s); a full-second wipe looks like a slideshow transition.
- Only on section changes: 2-4 per minute at most.
- The wipe's colours come from the piece's palette (tile wipes that use red/white/black on a red/white/black piece).

## Sound for it

Whoosh through the wipe, a hit as the new section lands; the bed can dip 2-3 dB under both.

## Common mistakes

- Using the wipe on every cut: it stops meaning "new section".
- A soft luma wipe (gradient) where the reference uses a hard-edged one: pick a mask with hard edges.
