# t100 Match cut into a screen or graphic

## What it is

A hero shot of a device (often 3 s of slow motion) hard-cuts at the same
angle into a full-frame screen capture or a flat illustration of what is on
it, usually on a tap click, sometimes with callouts. The viewer "enters"
the screen. App, laptop and education films.

## Build it in Zenvi

The faithful version tracks the screen and warps it to full frame
(`screen-quad-zoom-through`, not shipped). Today: push in toward the
screen centre, and hard cut on the tap to a full-frame graphic whose
layout matches what the screen showed.

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<phone in hand, screen facing camera>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 1.0, "end_seconds": 3.5, "track": "1"}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<hero>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.5, "value": 1.35}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<hero>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"timeline_seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"timeline_seconds": 2.5, "value": 1.35}]}
add_motion_block_tool {"block": "chart_build", "position_seconds": 2.5, "duration_seconds": 3.5, "placement": "full_frame"}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "ui_tap", "at_seconds": 2.5, "gain_db": -6}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "whoosh_short", "at_seconds": 2.45, "gain_db": -14}
master_audio_tool {"preset": "social", "iterate": true}
```

For an illustration instead of UI, `generate_image_and_add_to_timeline_tool`
with the device's on-screen subject and the same framing. Set
`location_x` / `location_y` keyframes so the push centres on the screen,
not the frame.

## Timing & taste rules

- Push 25-40 % over 2-3 s, ending on the cut; the screen fills at least a
  third of the frame on the last frame.
- The graphic's main element sits where the screen's was (same centre).
- Cut on the tap; the click on the cut frame.
- Hold the graphic 2-4 s before cutting back to the world.

## Sound for it

One ui_tap on the cut, a short whoosh peaking on it under the tap; the
graphic's own UI sounds after. The bed continues through.

## Common mistakes

- A screen at an angle cut to a flat graphic with no push: it reads as
  a random cut.
- The graphic's layout unrelated to what the screen showed.
- A dissolve: this is a hard cut.
