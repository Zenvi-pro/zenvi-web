# t101 Morph-chain scene joins

## What it is

The animated explainer as one continuous transformation: a bar chart becomes a head, a nucleus becomes target
rings, a ring becomes an orbit. Joins are morphs (and a fast zoom-through into an icon at section changes), so
there are almost no hard cuts; the shot detector sees one shot in 50 s.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Shape chain: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "shape_morph", "placement": "full_frame", "track": "1",
   "position_seconds": 0, "duration_seconds": 4, "params": {"shapes": ["circle", "ring", "hexagon", "star"],
   "style": "outline", "palette": "night"}}` (one shape per concept beat; `beat` sets seconds per shape).
2. Word chain: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "type_morph", "placement": "full_frame", "track": "1",
   "position_seconds": 4, "duration_seconds": 3, "params": {"words": ["atom", "cell", "life"], "palette": "night"}}`.
3. Section change between blocks: `apply_motion_transition_tool` `{"preset": "zoom_through", "track": "1",
   "time": 4}` (or `blur_dissolve`).
4. Sound every morph: `add_sfx_tool` `pop` (gain -18) per shape change and `whoosh_rise` on the zoom-through.

## Timing & taste rules

- One concept per 3-9 s beat; the morph itself 0.4-0.7 s with a long settle.
- Hard cuts under 20% of joins in an animated explainer.
- Keep one palette and one stroke weight for the whole chain.

## Sound for it

A soft pop or click on each morph, a whoosh on each push; all under the voice (the reference runs 24-36 cues/min).

## Common mistakes

- Morphing unrelated shapes without a narrative reason: the chain must say "this becomes that".
- Mixing filled and outline styles between scenes.
