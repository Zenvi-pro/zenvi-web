# t110 Zoom-through scene chain

## What it is

The camera pushes into an element of the shot (a laptop screen, an app
icon, a circular aperture, a doorway) and comes out in the next scene,
6-12 frames, motion blur at the swap, often a white flash at peak scale
and always a whoosh. Used at section changes, not on every cut.

## Build it in Zenvi

Each outgoing shot accelerates in before the cut so the transition
continues a move that already started; `zoom_through` flies into the
element at `focus_x`/`focus_y`.

```json
list_motion_transitions_tool {}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<laptop, screen visible>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 3, "end_seconds": 4.924, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<editing monitor>", "position_seconds": 1.924, "start_seconds": 5, "end_seconds": 6.848, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<phone in hand>", "position_seconds": 3.772, "start_seconds": 4, "end_seconds": 5.924, "track": "1"}
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<landscape>", "position_seconds": 5.696, "start_seconds": 6, "end_seconds": 7.6, "track": "1"}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<each outgoing shot>", "property": "scale_x", "points": [{"seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"seconds": 1.8, "value": 1.3, "ease": "ease_in_expo"}]}
set_keyframes_tool {"timeline_clip_id": "<each outgoing shot>", "property": "scale_y", "points": [{"seconds": 0, "value": 1}, {"seconds": 1.8, "value": 1.3, "ease": "ease_in_expo"}]}
apply_motion_transition_tool {"preset": "zoom_through", "clip1_id": "<laptop>", "focus_x": 0.68, "focus_y": 0.35}
apply_motion_transition_tool {"preset": "zoom_through", "clip1_id": "<monitor>", "focus_x": 0.55, "focus_y": 0.45}
apply_motion_transition_tool {"preset": "flash_white", "clip1_id": "<phone>"}
```

Keyframe `seconds` are from the clip's start and must stay inside it
(`set_keyframes_tool` refuses a point one frame past the end). The
faithful version flies *into* the screen with the next shot playing on
it; Zenvi cannot place footage on a screen quad (`screen-quad-zoom-through`),
so the screens should already show something, not a green or blank panel.

## Timing & taste rules

- Transition 0.27-0.4 s (8-12 frames); default `zoom_through` is 10.
- The push before the cut is exponential (`ease_in_expo`, 1.0 to 1.3):
  at 1.12 cubic the judge saw "static shots" and hard cuts.
- Focus on the element, not the frame centre: the screen, the icon.
- Three in a row at most, then a plain cut; the last join a
  `flash_white` onto the new section.
- Shots 1.5-2 s each, cuts on beats.

## Sound for it

A `whoosh_short` (-9 dB) peaking on every cut, panned alternately left
and right, a `whoosh_medium` plus `impact_punch` and `tail_bloom` on the
flash. End on a hit or a music phrase end, not mid-bar.

## Common mistakes

- Zooming into a blank or green screen: there is nothing to fly into.
- A push that only starts at the transition.
- A zoom-through on every cut of the film.
