# t113 Captions that dodge face or graphic

## What it is

Short phrase captions (2-5 words, about 0.8 s each, 3-3.5% of frame height) that sit wherever the picture is
free: above the illustration when it fills the bottom, below when it rises, beside the face on a talking head.
The graphic is never covered, so captions help instead of competing.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Captions from speech or script cues: `kinetic_captions_tool` `{"cues": [{"start": 0.2, "end": 2.1,
   "text": "A habit takes about sixty six days,"}, ...], "style": "documentary", "placement": "auto"}`.
   `placement: "auto"` reads each shot (subject matte on the CLI) and takes the calmest free spot.
2. A known graphic box: pass it as `avoid` (`[{"x": 0.5, "y": 0.35, "w": 0.6, "h": 0.5}]`) so captions go to
   the other band; for a graphic that moves between top and bottom, caption each section separately with
   `start_seconds`/`end_seconds` and its own `y`.
3. Nudge single cues with `edit_captions_tool`.

## Timing & taste rules

- 2-5 words per cue, changing on phrase boundaries (about every 0.8 s in fast explainers, 1.5-2.5 s in calm ones).
- Height 3-3.5% (documentary style default 0.064 of the short side on 16:9 is upper end; reduce with `text_size`).
- Caption box overlap with the face or active graphic: zero.

## Sound for it

None of its own.

## Common mistakes

- A fixed bottom band over a graphic that lives at the bottom.
- Moving captions every cue for no reason: switch bands only when the graphic moves.
