# t115 Fade-to-black reset with giant place title

## What it is

A breath between chapters: the subject sequence fades to black (1-2 s), the next place opens on a still wide,
four or five static wides of 2-2.5 s run on the place's ambience, and a light serif place name at about 25%
of frame height fades in over the last one. It resets attention and tells the viewer where they are.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Fade the outgoing shot: `fade_clip_tool` `{"clip_id": "<last>", "fade_out_seconds": 1.0}` and leave
   0.5-1 s of empty timeline (black) before the next clip.
2. The wides: `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` for 3-5 static wides, 2-2.5 s each; `fade_clip_tool`
   `fade_in_seconds: 0.8` on the first. Optional slow push: `set_clip_properties_tool` scale 1.0 to 1.04.
3. The place name: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "Ambato", "style": "editorial", "animation": "fade",
   "emphasis": "none", "text_size": 0.25, "ink": "#f2efe8", "position_seconds": <last wide + 0.4>,
   "duration_seconds": 3}`.
4. Sound: music fades with the picture; the new place's ambience (`add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "room_tone",
   "align": "start", "duration": <len>, "gain_db": -22}` or recorded wind/street) starts with the first wide.

## Timing & taste rules

- Black hold 0.5-2 s; wides static (or a 2-4% push), 2-2.5 s each; place name 20-30% of frame height, thin serif.
- No music for the reset; ambience carries it.
- Once per chapter at most; 1-3 per film.

## Sound for it

Silence then ambience; no whoosh, no hit. The quiet is the point.

## Common mistakes

- A bold sans place name: it reads like a sports graphic. Use a light serif.
- Handheld or moving wides here: the reset needs stillness.
