# t119 Keyword slam card

## What it is

The designed reset at the turn of an argument: footage drops out for a black (or flat accent) card carrying
one or two words at 12-25% of frame height that land with an overshoot or a slam, on a sub drop, while the
music dips. "TRUST", "Until...", "THE MONEY." Once per piece, between 33% and 68% of the runtime.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The card: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "TRUST", "style": "stack", "animation": "slam",
   "background": "#0b0b0b", "accent": "#e1261c", "emphasis": ["TRUST"], "text_size": 0.24,
   "position_seconds": <turn>, "duration_seconds": 1.6}`. Two words max: longer lines push words off frame
   at slam sizes.
2. The hit: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "sub_drop", "at_seconds": <turn>}` plus `{"recipe": "impact_slam",
   "at_seconds": <turn>, "gain_db": -10}`.
3. The dip: `automate_volume_tool` `{"role": "music", "shape": "dip", "start_seconds": <turn - 0.1>,
   "end_seconds": <card end>, "db": -14}`.
4. Numbers instead of words: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "counter", ...}` over the same card.

## Timing & taste rules

- 1.2-2 s on screen; one per piece (two in a 90 s essay at most).
- Word 3-4.5x the caption size (12-18% of height vs 4-5% captions).
- Card colour = the piece's one accent or pure black; type in white or the accent, nothing else on the card.

## Sound for it

Sub drop on the first frame, an impact layered on it, the bed dipped 12-14 dB for the card's length and
back on the next shot.

## Common mistakes

- Slam cards every few seconds: they stop being a turn.
- A sentence on the card: the slam only works on one or two words.
- Forgetting the dip: the hit fights the music.
