# t120 Letter-by-letter text reveal

## What it is

Text that appears as it would be written: a quote typed on a parchment card with a typewriter clacking, a
title revealed left to right as the runner crosses the frame. After fast imagery it slows everything down
and makes the viewer read.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. Typed card: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "We did not know it was the last summer.", "style":
   "editorial", "animation": "typewriter", "stagger_seconds": 0.06, "background": "#efe6d2", "ink": "#2a2620", "emphasis": "none",
   "text_size": 0.09, "position_seconds": <t>, "duration_seconds": 6}`.
2. Keystrokes: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "typing_burst", "at_seconds": <t + 0.05>, "align": "start",
   "duration": <letters x stagger>}` (40 letters x 0.06 s = 2.4 s; without `stagger_seconds` the line types in
   about 1.3 s and the clatter outlasts it); a bell or `keyboard_key` on the last character.
3. Music drops to one sustained note: `automate_volume_tool` `{"role": "music", "shape": "dip", ...,
   "db": -10}`; paper grain `apply_look_preset_tool` `film_grain_16mm_classic`.
4. Title over moving footage (left-to-right wipe): an `add_title_tool` title plus `add_shape_mask_tool`
   with `keyframes` growing a rectangle from the left, timed to the subject's crossing.
5. Mono decode alternative: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "text_scramble", ...}`.

## Timing & taste rules

- `add_kinetic_title_tool` types at a fixed pace (about 30 characters per second; `stagger_seconds` did not
  slow it in the 2026-10-28 render), so a 40-letter line is typed in about 1.3 s: keep the keystroke burst to
  that length and plan the hold. Real typing speed control is missing (gap `typewriter-pace`).
- Typing speed 12-20 characters per second reads best; hold the finished line at least 2 s.
- One serif face, about 4% height for quotes; parchment or black card, nothing else on screen.
- Use once or twice per piece; it is a change of tempo.

## Sound for it

Keystrokes in sync with letters (the burst approximates), music down to a pad, a soft bell on the last letter.

## Common mistakes

- Typing a long paragraph: cut it to one line.
- Keystrokes that run after the typing stops: set the burst `duration` to the typing length.
