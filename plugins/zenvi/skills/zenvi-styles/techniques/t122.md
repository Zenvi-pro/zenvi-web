# t122 Overshoot pop-in word or letter card

## What it is

The kids-vocabulary beat: the child (or narrator) says the noun, about 3 frames later the word pops in, 0 to
115% scale in about 4 frames, settles to 100% in 3, idles for about 1 s, and cuts away. A confetti or sparkle
burst and a chime land on the pop frame.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The word, after the spoken noun: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "GRAPES", "style": "creator",
   "animation": "pop", "emphasis": ["GRAPES"], "accent": "#ffcc00", "y": 0.62,
   "position_seconds": <word end + 0.1>, "duration_seconds": 1.2}` (one word, the emphasis face).
2. From speech: `kinetic_captions_tool` with `emphasis` on the nouns and `animation: "pop"`.
3. Burst behind it: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "particle_burst", "position_seconds": <same>,
   "duration_seconds": 1.0, "params": {"shape": "confetti", "origin": [{"x": 0.5, "y": 0.62}], "count": 60}}`.
4. Sound: `add_sfx_tool` `pop` on the pop frame and `sparkle` 0.05 s later at -16 dB.
5. A custom snap on any clip (logo, emoji): `set_keyframes_tool` `scale_x`/`scale_y` with
   `"ease": "ease_out_back"`, points 0 at 0 s to 1.0 at 0.2 s.

## Timing & taste rules

- Word appears 0.1 s (3 frames) after the spoken word, never before it.
- Pop 4 frames up, 3 settle; hold 1.0-1.1 s; hard-cut exit.
- Large and simple: bubble or condensed caps, high-contrast fill; on 9:16 at least 7% of height.

- 9:16 projects: use portrait footage (`gather_stock_footage_tool` `"orientation": "portrait"`) or fill landscape
  clips with `set_clip_property_tool` `{"property": "scale", "value": 0}` (crop); by default a landscape clip is
  fitted with black bars, which the judge reads as an unformatted draft.

## Sound for it

A pop or chime exactly on the pop frame, a sparkle tail; each unit can use a different variation (`seed`).

## Common mistakes

- Word before the voice: it spoils the "name it" game.
- Linear scale-in: without overshoot it reads as a zoom, not a pop.
