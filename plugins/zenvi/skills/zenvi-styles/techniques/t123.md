# t123 Restrained title card on black

## What it is

The quiet title: small centred caps or a serif line (3.5-9% height) on black or over a plate, held 2-6 s,
fading in and out or dissolving into the first image. In long-form it arrives after the cold open (about
1.5 min in), and as attribution ("Film (year)") it never repeats the VO.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The card: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "The Last Ice Merchant", "style": "documentary",
   "animation": "fade", "emphasis": "none", "background": "#000000", "text_size": 0.05,
   "position_seconds": <t>, "duration_seconds": 4.5}`; a serif line instead: `"style": "editorial"`.
   Two lines (title + credit): `add_title_tool` `{"template": "Standard_1", "text": "...",
   "subtitle": "...", "background_color": "#000000", "fade_in_seconds": 0.8, "fade_out_seconds": 0.8}`.
2. Into picture: `add_transition_tool` `{"track": "1", "position_seconds": <card end>, "duration_seconds": 1.2,
   "transition": "common/fade", "direction": "in"}` on the first shot, or `fade_clip_tool` on it.
3. Music enters on the card and the previous room tone dies on the same frame: `automate_volume_tool`.

## Timing & taste rules

- 3.5-9% of frame height, light weight, wide tracking for caps; nothing else on the card.
- Hold 2-6 s; fades 0.5-1 s; a 1-2 s dissolve into the first image.
- Title after the hook in long-form; in shorts the first 2 s belong to the image, not the title.

## Sound for it

Silence or music entering on the card's first frame; no hit, no whoosh.

## Common mistakes

- Big bold titles: it turns into a trailer card.
- Fading the card while the music is still the previous scene's: kill the old sound on the cut.
