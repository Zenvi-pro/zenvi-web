# t125 Staccato keyword list punch

## What it is

"Internet. Phones. Maps. Everything." Four or five single words, each about 0.8 s on screen at 8-16% of frame
height, each over its own B-roll insert and each with a bass thump. A 3-4 s burst that turns a spoken list
into rhythm, then releases into a longer shot.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. One insert per word: `gather_stock_footage_tool` `{"queries": ["city lights aerial", "phone map",
   "map on wall", "earth from space"]}`, then `add_clip_to_timeline_tool` 0.8 s windows back to back.
2. One word per insert: `add_kinetic_title_tool` `{"text": "Internet", "style": "stack", "animation": "slam",
   "emphasis": "none", "text_size": 0.16, "position_seconds": <t>, "duration_seconds": 0.8}`.
3. A thump per word: `add_sfx_tool` `{"recipe": "impact_thud", "at_seconds": <t>, "seed": <i>, "gain_db": -10}`
   (vary the seed so the hits are not identical).
4. Release: the shot after the list is 2-4 s long.
5. On music: snap the word times to beats from `detect_beats_tool` (every beat or every second beat).

## Timing & taste rules

- 0.7-0.9 s per word, identical spacing; 4-5 words; one burst per piece.
- Words in the piece's display face at 8-16% height, white or the one accent.
- The VO says the list in the same rhythm, or there is no VO under it.

## Sound for it

A low thump per word (same family, varied seed), bed slightly ducked; a longer hit or silence on the release.

## Common mistakes

- Uneven spacing: the punch only works as a metronome.
- Long words or phrases: one word each.
