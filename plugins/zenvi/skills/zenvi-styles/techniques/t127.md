# t127 Strobe text stack and occlusion open

## What it is

Two moves of one reel. Opener: a bold lowercase claim (about 7 % of frame
height) sits in the scene and the walking subject crosses in front of it.
Punchline: 10-12 copies of one line appear down the frame, each on its own
drum hit, then collapse to one centred line in under 1 s.

## Build it in Zenvi

Opener: `text_behind_subject_tool` makes the subject matte and the stack
itself (footage, text, cut-out person on top).

```json
add_clip_to_timeline_tool {"file_id": "<person walking across frame>", "position_seconds": 0, "start_seconds": 0.5, "end_seconds": 2.905, "track": "1"}
text_behind_subject_tool {"clip_id": "<walk clip>", "text": "work moves here", "size": 0.13, "y": 0.42}
```

Stack: one `add_kinetic_title_tool` per copy (no echo tool yet), all
ending on the same frame; the first carries the dark card. Start them on
the music's 16th notes (0.12 s at 125 BPM), one `hit_transient` each.

```json
add_kinetic_title_tool {"text": "ship it today", "position_seconds": 2.405, "duration_seconds": 1.2, "style": "documentary", "animation": "fade", "stagger_seconds": 0, "text_size": 0.065, "y": 0.08, "ink": "#FFFFFF", "background": "#0B0B0F"}
add_kinetic_title_tool {"text": "ship it today", "position_seconds": 2.525, "duration_seconds": 1.08, "style": "documentary", "animation": "fade", "stagger_seconds": 0, "text_size": 0.065, "y": 0.16}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "hit_transient", "at_seconds": 2.525, "gain_db": -17, "seed": 1}
add_kinetic_title_tool {"text": "ship it today", "position_seconds": 3.605, "duration_seconds": 1.7, "style": "creator", "animation": "slam", "emphasis": ["today"], "accent": "#FF5A36", "text_size": 0.09, "y": 0.5, "background": "#0B0B0F"}
add_sfx_tool {"recipe": "impact_punch", "at_seconds": 3.605, "gain_db": -8}
```

Repeat the copy call with `y` 0.08 to 0.88 in 0.08 steps. Each title
needs `duration_seconds` 0.4 or more, so the last copies must start at
least 0.4 s before the collapse: 11 copies at 0.08-0.1 s steps fit in a
1.2 s stack; at 0.12 s steps use 8 copies.

## Timing & taste rules

- Stack 0.8-1.2 s, a new copy every 2-4 frames, each on a transient.
- Collapse on a downbeat: every copy off on the same frame, the single
  line on with a slam, held at least 1.5 s with a hit.
- The opener text sits behind the head or torso and the subject must
  pass in front of it within the first second.
- Lowercase, one bold sans, white; one accent word in the payoff only.

## Sound for it

One quiet `hit_transient` (-17 dB, a new `seed` each) per copy, an
`impact_punch` plus `tail_bloom` on the collapse, a short whoosh into the
stack. The music's drum fill should be under the stack.

## Common mistakes

- Copies arriving on a timer instead of the drum hits.
- A flat black void for the stack after a live-action open: keep a dark
  blurred frame of the street under it (the judge's second fix).
- Ending on the payoff line without a hit and a hold.
