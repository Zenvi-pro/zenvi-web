# t129 Live play-by-play narration of moving data

## What it is

Data as sport: while the bubbles or bars move, the narrator commentates ("and here it goes, down, down...")
at about 145 wpm with rising pitch, naming what moves as it moves. The cut to the capture is timed to the
fastest movement. Produced narration elsewhere sits nearer 105 wpm: the contrast is the technique.

## Build it in Zenvi

1. The moving data: `add_motion_block_tool` `{"block": "chart_build", ...}` or a supplied capture.
2. The call: short VO lines, one per state change. TTS: `generate_tts_and_add_to_timeline_tool`
   `{"text": "...", "speed": 1.15}` per line, placed at each bar's growth; a recorded human read is far better.
3. Tighten: `remove_silence_tool` on the read; `compress_tool` on the voice for energy.
4. Sync: `set_clip_speed_tool` on the capture so its fastest segment lands on the excited line;
   check line times with `get_transcript_tool`.

## Timing & taste rules

- About 140-150 wpm during the play-by-play vs about 105 wpm elsewhere.
- Each line starts within 0.2 s of the movement it names.

## Sound for it

Voice forward and compressed; bed low or absent; no cue sounds that step on the call.

## Common mistakes

- Flat TTS: generated voices cannot perform excitement; prefer the user's own recording.
